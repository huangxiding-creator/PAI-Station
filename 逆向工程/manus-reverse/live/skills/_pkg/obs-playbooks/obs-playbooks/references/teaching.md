# Teaching (Card G1)

## Card G1 — Explain and demonstrate OBS

**Use when:** the user asks what Manus can do with OBS, wants one setting/object explained, or wants a narrated demonstration in their actual client.

**Inputs:** learning outcome, exact client/OS/version, disposable fixture, current hardware/state, lesson start/end state, secrets to exclude, and repeat checklist.

**Primary channel:** guidance when no live state is required; D1 for one current-build lesson only after its fixture and live replay pass. F1, A1, and H1 teach upstream analysis/asset/site work separately.

**Escalation trigger:** production Profile/Collection, secret-bearing UI, public output, unsupported hardware/plugin, unavailable control, or a lesson without a reversible fixture.

**Hybrid handoff:** F1 prepares a synthetic asset/log/blueprint; D1 demonstrates one named OBS object; a short guide records decisions, variables, verification, and rollback.

**Workflow:**

1. Define one lesson outcome rather than a broad tour.
2. Establish exact build and disposable start state; remove secret/live-output risk.
3. Observe the current control and record the candidate procedure.
4. Demonstrate one bounded change and narrate why it matters.
5. Save/reopen or otherwise verify the object.
6. Replay from the starting fixture and deliver the repeat checklist.

**Verification:** exact client/OS/version, fixture, observed start/end state, saved/reopened object, successful replay, and no secret or public action in recording.

**Failure and recovery:** restore the fixture and keep the lesson as guidance/HOLD when the current control or postcondition cannot be proved.

**Record as skill:** one lesson per recording. Parameterize Profile/Collection, object, source, target, and values; never hard-code credentials, coordinates, hardware, or production names.

**CU display:** yes only after that exact lesson passes fixture, replay, and recovery.
