摘要 
 
随着中国工程咨询服务供给侧结构性改革进一步深化，现行的碎片化工程咨
询服务供给模式矛盾，社会对综合性、跨阶段、一体化的全过程工程咨询服务需
求日益增强。工程总承包模式的兴起给工程咨询企业带来了全新的挑战，同时也
带来了全新的机遇。为了满足委托人多样化的委托需求，提高市场核心竞争力，
工程咨询企业亟需转型升级。 
基于上述背景，本文研究界定出重构知识价值链是工程咨询企业进行转型升
级的有效途径，并将知识价值链作为研究对象，围绕工程总承包项目投资管控咨
询展开研究。首先，通过扎根理论对试点工程咨询企业所进行的工程总承包项目
的投资管控咨询案例进行编码分析，经过对22 份案例报告的细致分析之后，共
贴出50 个工作范畴。在范畴化“组配”过程结束后，最终形成了11 个主范畴，
进而总结概况出4 个核心范畴。通过将工程总承包项目的咨询服务与传统的咨询
服务业务进行对比分析，找到适应于新模式的增值项目，并基于ESIA 法通过取
消、简化、整合和自动化四个步骤对对全过程投资管控咨询业务流程进行了重新
设计。然后，基于企业层面和项目层面的知识分类，以各阶段的知识成果文件为
依据，归纳分析得出工程咨询企业在工程总承包项目各咨询阶段知识活动所需要
的知识。进而对知识主体进行研究，将工程咨询企业知识主体与知识进行匹配，
并基于投资管控咨询业务流程，进一步构建出工程总承包项目全过程投资管控咨
询知识流模型，对投资管控咨询产生的知识进行标识。最后，在上述研究的基础
上，站在企业层面和项目层面对工程总承包项目全过程投资管控咨询价值链和知
识链模型进行构建。基于企业业务流程研究视角下的知识价值链理论，并对价值
链和知识链进行耦合，互动融合为双链复合型知识价值链，并通过X 工程咨询
企业案例对知识价值链模型进行验证分析。 
总体而言，本文基于扎根理论对案例进行分析作为业务识别的工具手段，之
后探讨工程咨询企业基于核心业务开展的知识活动，并最终落实到工程咨询企业
知识价值链模型的构建，为传统造价咨询公司适应建筑业新环境带来的冲击，提
高工程咨询企业核心竞争力，促使工程咨询企业转型升级带来了帮助。 
 
关键词：工程咨询企业，工程总承包项目，全过程投资管控咨询，知识价值链
  
 
Abstract 
 
With the further deepening of structural reforms on the supply side of China's 
engineering consulting services, the current supply model of fragmented engineering 
consulting services is contradictory, and the society's demand for comprehensive, 
cross-stage, and integrated whole-process engineering consulting services is 
increasing. 
Based on the above background, this paper defines that the reconstruction of 
knowledge value chain is an effective way for engineering consulting enterprises to 
transform and upgrade, and takes the knowledge value chain as the research object to 
conduct research around the investment management and control consulting of 
engineering general contracting projects. First of all, through the grounded theory, the 
investment management and control consulting cases of the general contracting 
projects carried out by the pilot engineering consulting companies are coded and 
analyzed. After a detailed analysis of 22 case reports, a total of 50 work categories are 
posted. After the categorization "assembly" process is over, 11 main categories are 
finally formed, and then 4 core categories are summarized. By comparing and 
analyzing the consulting services of the general contracting project with the traditional 
consulting service business, find out the value-added projects that adapt to the new 
model, and based on the ESIA method, through the four steps of Eliminate, Simplify, 
Integrate and Automate, the whole process of investment management and control 
consulting. Then, based on the knowledge classification of enterprise level and project 
level, and based on the knowledge achievement documents of each stage, the 
knowledge required by engineering consulting enterprises in each consulting stage of 
the general contracting project is obtained by induction and analysis. Then research 
the knowledge subject, match the knowledge subject and knowledge of the 
engineering consulting enterprise, and further construct the investment management 
and control consulting knowledge flow model of the whole process of the general 
contracting project based on the investment management and control consulting 
business process, and analyze the knowledge generated by the investment 
management and control consulting. Finally, on the basis of the above research, the 
value chain and knowledge chain model of the whole process investment management 
and control consulting of the engineering procurement construction project are 
  
constructed at the enterprise level and the project level. Based on the knowledge value 
chain theory from the perspective of enterprise business process research, the value 
chain and knowledge chain are coupled, interactively fused into a double-chain 
composite knowledge value chain, and the knowledge value chain model is verified 
and analyzed through X engineering consulting enterprise cases. 
In general, this paper analyzes the case based on grounded theory as a tool for 
business identification, then discusses the knowledge activities carried out by 
engineering consulting companies based on their core business, and finally 
implements the construction of the knowledge value chain model of engineering 
consulting companies. It has brought help for traditional cost consulting companies to 
adapt to the impact brought by the new environment of the construction industry, 
improve the core competitiveness of engineering consulting companies, and promote 
the transformation and upgrading of engineering consulting companies. 
 
Key words：Engineering consulting company, Engineering procurement construction, 
Whole-process investment management and control consulting, Knowledge value 
chain
