How to define Loss Mitigation for the purpose of
this paper?
Pre and Post loss measures which help to reduce the actually
sustained loss (PD and BI) suffered by the Insured and with an impact for Insurers
Not: 
o pure coverage analysis (e.g. application of limits, exclusions)
o Subrogation
