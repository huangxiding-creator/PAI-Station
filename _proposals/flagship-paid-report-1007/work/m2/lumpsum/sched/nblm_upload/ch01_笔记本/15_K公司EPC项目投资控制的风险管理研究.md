I 
摘  要 
目前，随着国家大力推进工程总承包模式，工程总承包（EPC）模式在我国正逐步成为我
国建筑行业的主流引领模式。由于国内的施工总承包单位对工程总承包（EPC）管理模式认识
不足，管理水平与国际一流管理水平相比有着相当大的差距，造成了很多工程总承包项目承包
单位亏损或业主单位在合约规定的工期内无法得到满意的交付成果。建筑工程项目实施进程中
施工承包商管理能力的不成熟直接影响项目的工期、质量，最终导致项目成本管控混乱，从而
产生的工期延长、质量降低都大大增加了建设业主单位投资控制风险和交付风险。因此，在建
设业主单位无法完全放手EPC 项目投资控制的风险管理的当下，主动地参与项目全过程的投
资控制相关风险管理就是十分必要的。 
本文以工程总承包项目实施的各阶段顺序为风险分析的主线，以工程投资控制的相关风险
为风险识别要素，结合K 公司已建成的工程总承包项目实例为研究样本，研究建设方业主单
位工程总承包项目投资控制的风险管理体系。通过收集K 公司已完成工程总承包项目的相关
文件进行整理、归纳后，运用检查表法、问卷调查法通过专家进行风险识别，对风险因素发生
的概率和影响大小进行比较选择评价，使用层次分析法对评价结果进行赋值和排序，从而从定
量分析角度得出各个风险影响程度等级。通过对项目风险主要影响因素的总结，结合项目经验，
提出合理的应对措施，并提出部分应对方法在在建项目中实施的效果。降低工程总承包建设业
主单位的项目投资控制风险，提高项目风险管控能力，为其他建设业主单位的工程总承包项目
投资控制风险管理工作提供参考借鉴。 
 
关键词：工程总承包，投资控制，风险管理，检查表法，层次分析法

ABSTRACT 
At present, as the country vigorously promotes the EPC mode, the EPC mode is gradually 
becoming the mainstream leading mode in my country's construction industry. Due to the lack of 
understanding of the EPC management mode by domestic construction general contractors, there is a 
considerable gap between the management level and the international first-class management level, 
resulting in many EPC project contracting units losing money or the owner unit being unable to obtain 
satisfactory results within the construction period stipulated in the contract. Deliver results. During the 
implementation of construction projects, the immature management capabilities of construction 
contractors directly affect the project duration and quality, ultimately leading to confusion in project 
cost control. The resulting extension of the duration and lower quality greatly increase the investment 
control risks and delivery risks of the construction owner. . Therefore, at a time when the construction 
owner cannot completely let go of the risk management of EPC project investment control, it is very 
necessary to actively participate in the investment control-related risk management of the entire 
project process. 
This article takes the sequence of each stage of EPC project implementation as the main line of 
risk analysis, takes the risks related to engineering investment control as risk identification elements, 
and combines the completed EPC project examples of K Company as a research sample to study the 
construction owner's EPC project investment control Risk management system. After collecting and 
sorting out the relevant documents of the EPC project that Company K has completed, experts use the 
checklist method and questionnaire survey method to identify risks, compare and select the evaluation 
of the probability and impact of risk factors, and use the analytic hierarchy process to The evaluation 
results are assigned and sorted to obtain each risk impact level from a quantitative analysis 
perspective. By summarizing the main influencing factors of project risks and combining project 
experience, reasonable countermeasures are proposed, and the effects of some countermeasures 
implemented in projects under construction are proposed. Reduce project investment risks of EPC 
construction owners, improve project risk management and control capabilities, and provide reference 
for other construction owners’ EPC project investment risk management work. 
 
Key words: engineering procurement construction, investment control, risk management, checklist 
method, analytic hierarchy process
