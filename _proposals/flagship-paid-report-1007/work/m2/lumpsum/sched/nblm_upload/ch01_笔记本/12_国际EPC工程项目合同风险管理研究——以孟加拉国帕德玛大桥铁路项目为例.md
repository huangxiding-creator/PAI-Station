I 
摘    要 
本文以孟加拉国帕德玛大桥铁路项目为研究对象，针对国际EPC 工程项目合同风
险的复杂性与动态性特征，系统构建覆盖施工方视角的风险管理框架。通过建立多维风
险框架，使用定性与定量相结合的风险识别方法，旨在解决跨国工程中风险识别模糊、
评估主观性及防控策略适配性不足的难题，为提升中国承包商在全球基建市场的风险韧
性提供方法论支撑。 
通过文献分析法筛选中国知网近三年、高引用文献20 篇，结合实地调研访谈（覆
盖11 个部门30 名专家），提取初始风险因素40 项，删除争议性指标5 项，最终形成包
含政治、商务、经济、环境、技术及管理6 个一级指标、35 个二级指标的风险评估体系。
研究提出了改进的AHP-TOPSIS 风险评估综合模型：在层次分析法（AHP）中引入区间
化语义约束概念，通过数学规划模型确定最优判断矩阵，降低主观偏差；在TOPSIS 模
型中优化正负理想解获取值，改进效益型与成本型指标的标准化公式，实现安全、质量、
进度、成本四大目标的独立评估与耦合计算。 
基于孟加拉国帕德玛大桥铁路项目（合同额31.39 亿美元，工期1643 天）的实证分
析表明，项目整体风险等级为2 级（较低风险），但关键风险呈现显著分化——气候水
文（权重0.072）与自然灾害（0.056）对进度目标影响显著，政局稳定（0.047）、政策连
续性（0.038）、通货膨胀（0.051）与汇率波动（0.040）对成本目标冲击突出。研究进一
步揭示风险要素与工程目标的映射关系：经济风险通过汇率波动与税收政策调整直接影
响成本控制，环境风险通过雨季停工与物流延误传导至进度目标，政治风险通过政策中
断与社会动荡系统威胁项目安全。基于此，提出“规避-转移-减轻-自留”防控策略：通
过政府协议锁定政策稳定性、优化宗教敏感期施工计划实现风险规避；采用主权担保信
用证与工程一切险转移金融与灾害风险；应用模块化施工技术与BIM 协同平台减轻气
候影响与设计变更；设立应急基金自留低概率可控风险。 
本研究的理论创新在于提出“多维风险因素分析框架”，改进的AHP-TOPSIS 模型
增强权重计算可信度，提升了标准值计算效率。实践层面，揭示了经济风险对成本目标
的影响、环境风险对进度目标的传导效应；构建“风险管控”协同机制，为“一带一路”
背景下国际EPC 工程合同风险管理提供了一定参考价值。

Study on Contract Risk Management of International EPC Projects 
——The Padma Bridge Railway Project in Bangladesh 
Abstract 
This study focuses on the Padma Bridge Railway Project in Bangladesh to address the 
complexity and dynamic nature of contract risks in international EPC projects. It systematically 
constructs a comprehensive risk management framework from the contractor's perspective. By 
establishing a multidimensional risk framework and employing a combination of qualitative 
and quantitative risk identification methods, the study aims to address the challenges of 
ambiguous risk identification, subjective risk assessment, and insufficient adaptability of 
prevention strategies in cross-border engineering projects. This research provides 
methodological support for enhancing Chinese contractors' risk resilience in the global 
infrastructure market. 
A risk assessment system was developed through literature analysis (prioritizing high-
impact publications) and field research (including expert interviews). Initial risk factors were 
refined via the Delphi method, resulting in a hierarchical structure comprising six primary 
indicators (political, commercial, economic, environmental, technical, and managerial risks) 
and 35 secondary indicators. An enhanced AHP-TOPSIS model was proposed: interval-based 
semantic constraints were integrated into the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) to optimize 
judgment matrices and reduce subjectivity, while the TOPSIS model was refined by redefining 
ideal solutions and improving normalization formulas for benefit- and cost-type indicators, 
enabling independent evaluation and integrated analysis of safety, quality, schedule, and cost 
objectives. 
Empirical analysis of the Padma Bridge Railway Project revealed an overall risk level of 
Grade 2, with critical risks exhibiting distinct impacts. Environmental risks  significantly 
affected schedule objectives, while political risks and economic risks disproportionately 
threatened safety and cost targets. The study further identified risk-objective linkages: 
economic risks directly influenced cost control through fiscal policy shifts, environmental risks 
disrupted schedules via seasonal delays, and political risks systematically jeopardized project 
safety through socio-political instability. A four-tiered risk mitigation strategy ("avoidance-
transfer-mitigation-retention") was formulated, emphasizing policy stabilization, financial risk 
transfer through sovereign guarantees, technical adaptations to environmental challenges, and 
contingency reserves for low-probability risks. 
Theoretically, this research introduces a multidimensional risk analysis framework and 
enhances the credibility of weight calculations through the improved AHP-TOPSIS model. 
Practically, it elucidates the cascading effects of economic and environmental risks on project 
objectives and establishes a synergistic risk governance mechanism. These contributions
