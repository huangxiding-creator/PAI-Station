浙江大学硕士学位论文                                                                    摘要 
II 
摘要 
核电工程一般采用EPC总承包固定总价合同，总承包商承担设计、采购、施
工的全部责任和风险，履行进度、成本、质量、HSE、合同等要素管理职责。核
电EPC工程建设阶段多要素管理离散，缺乏统一价值衡量准则和管理机制，导致
资源管理分散和浪费、重点任务清单失真、交付成果价值度不高等系列问题。 
本文在充分汲取国内外文献资料理论成果基础上，根据核电EPC工程管理特
征和优势，以实现多要素协同管理和统一管理价值评判准则为目的，设计“要素
-计划-实施”三维结构模型，重点研究模型构建，主要内容如下： 
1）要素管理为知识维，主要研究要素管理关键因素和要素价值评判准则。
利用第一性原理，提出要素特征属性概念，以特征属性作为要素管理活动的价值
标签。运用调查问卷法、专家经验法和SPSS26.0统计分析，形成要素特征属性集、
多要素管理价值指标集，涉及进度、成本、质量、HSE、技术等17类要素、51
项要素特征属性，要素评判准则分三个维度：目标要素、能力建设、干系人管理，
共包括18项一级指标和76项二级指标。 
2）计划管理为时间维，构建三层级计划架构：核电工程建设四阶段-EPC一
体化计划-要素管理计划。本文重点研究工程施工建造阶段的多要素综合计划，
包括EPC一体化计划和要素管理计划。分析综合计划架构、内在关联链路，实现
综合计划的时限、逻辑、关联管理。通过工程管理特征和管理要素的关联分析，
梳理出常用的要素管理计划，其要素管理活动是价值评价和决策对象。 
3）多属性决策管理方法实施过程为主逻辑维，用于赋予要素管理活动价值，
对管理活动作出价值评估和决策。赋值涉及两个维度：要素特征属性和价值评判
准则。本文构建两个维度决策矩阵，分析要素管理活动与决策矩阵对应关系，运
用三角模糊TOPSIS多属性决策管理方法和主、客观赋权法，对要素管理活动作
价值评估和决策管理，根据决策结果评价管理机制效用。 
本文研究核电EPC工程多要素管理三维模型及拓扑关系，提出一种多要素管
理价值量化和决策机制，以核电EPC工程S项目为实例，选取48个典型要素管理
活动为价值评估对象，将管理机制应用于工程。 
关键词：核电EPC工程；霍尔三维模型；多要素管理；TOPSIS多属性决策
方法.

Abstract 
Projects of nuclear power engineering are used to adopting EPC (design, 
procurement, construction) general contracting model with fixed-total-cost price 
contracts. EPC general contractors almost undertake all the responsibilities and risks 
of design, procurement, construction, and perform management for multi-factors, 
such as schedule, cost, quality, HSE, and contract etc. In the construction stage of 
nuclear power EPC engineering, the discretization of factor management ,lacking 
unified value measurement criteria and management mechanism, leads to a series of 
problems such as scattered and wasted resource management, distorted key work list, 
and low value of delivered results. 
Based on the theoretical achievements of domestic and foreign literature, and 
according to the management characteristics and advantages of nuclear power EPC 
engineering, 
this 
paper 
designs 
a 
three-dimensional 
structure 
model 
of 
"factor-plan-implementation" for the purpose of realizing multi-factor collaborative 
management and unified value evaluation criteria. The model building is the main 
research contents as follows:  
1) Factor management is a knowledge dimension, mainly studying the key factors 
and multi-factor management value evaluation criteria. By using the first principle, 
the concept is proposed about factor characteristic attribute as the value label of factor 
management activities. By using questionnaire method, expert experience method and 
SPSS26.0 statistical analysis, the factor characteristic attribute set and multi-factor 
management value index set are formed, involving 17 categories of factors, 51 factor 
characteristic attributes, such as progress, cost, quality, HSE, technology. The 
evaluation criteria of factors are divided into three dimensions: target factors, capacity 
building, stakeholder management, including 18 first-level and 76 second-level 
indicators.

2) The plan management is the time dimension, and a three-level plan structure is 
constructed: four stages of nuclear power engineering construction - EPC integration 
plan - factor management plan. The multi-factor comprehensive plan in the 
construction stage of the project is concerned , including EPC integration plan and 
factor management plan. The plan structure and internal link are analyzed to realize 
the time limit and logical management. Through the analysis of the relationship 
between engineering characteristics and factor management, the common factor 
management plan is sorted out, and the factor activities are the objects of value 
evaluation and decision-making. 
 3) The implementation process of multi-attribute decision-making management 
method is the main logical dimension, which is used to give value to the factor 
activities through making value evaluation and decision-making. The assignment 
involves two dimensions: factor characteristic attribute and value evaluation criteria . 
By construction of two-dimensional decision matrix, and the analysis of the 
corresponding relationship between factor activities and decision matrix, triangular 
fuzzy TOPSIS multi-attribute decision-making management (MADM) method and 
subjective and objective weighting method is used for the value evaluation and 
decision-making management of factor activities. According to the decision results , 
management utility is evaluated.  
This paper establishes a multi-factor management value quantification and 
decision-making mechanism about nuclear power EPC engineering through studing 
model three dimensionals and their topological relationship. Taking S project of 
nuclear power engineering as an example, 48 typical factor activities are selected as 
the value evaluation objects for the apllication. 
 Key words: Nuclear power EPC engineering; Hall 3-D model; Multi-factor 
management ; MADM method based on TOPSIS.
