 I
EPC 总承包工程项目管理成熟度评价研究
--以电力设计院为视角
摘要
随着2030 年“碳达峰”与2060 年“碳中和”目标的提出，我国开始
持续推进产业结构与能源结构调整，加速规划建设大型风电光伏基地，扩
大电力建设工程EPC 总承包市场规模。此前，电力体制改革不断促使电力
设计院以“主辅分离”为契机直面市场，向EPC 模式转型，发挥以设计为
龙头的天然优势对EPC 总承包工程项目的统筹作用从而开拓新市场。如何
在电力机制深化改革趋势下寻求发展稳定平衡点并抓住新能源政策的机遇
为企业在EPC 总承包市场中的成长提供动力，在加速转型的同时不断增强
市场竞争力是电力设计院亟待突破的困境之一。在此背景下，本文站在电
力设计院视角，对EPC 总承包工程项目管理成熟度评价模型进行研究，提
出能够提升其项目管理能力的对策与建议，主要研究内容及结论如下：
（1）基于扎根理论与问卷调查法，站在电力设计院视角构建EPC 总承
包工程项目管理成熟度评价指标体系，包含EPC 项目管理组织、EPC 项目
管理要素、EPC 项目管理过程3 个分类维度下的18 个要素和56 个指标，
保证指标体系完整性的前提下突出电力设计院作为EPC 总承包商的指向性。
（2）运用COWA-DEMATEL 组合方法进行指标赋权，既能够综合独
立权重与关联权重降低少数极端数值对计算结果的不良影响，又分析了指
标间的相互影响性。选取三维成熟度模型OPM3 作为基础模型，采用非对
称贴近度原则代替最大隶属度原则改进传统物元可拓模型，在保证成熟度
评价全面性的基础上提高模型精度。
（3）对YHP 煤电一体化电厂新建工程EPC 总承包项目展开实例分析，
得到成熟度评价等级为定义级，有轻微向重复级发展的趋势，评价结果与
实际相符；采用CSA-BP 神经网络对评价结果进行预测，一方面验证本文
所搭建的成熟度评价模型的可行性，另一方面为动态预测EPC 总承包工程
 II
项目管理成熟度等级提供理论参考。
（4）结合指标综合权重与成熟度评价模型结果，发现该电力设计院在
EPC 总承包工程项目管理方面尚未成熟，进而从提升风险防控能力、完善
沟通与协调机制和采购管理机制、重视财务管理与施工管理工作、关注工
程进度推进、强化EPC 职能建设、转变传统设计观念、提升服务意识等方
面给出建议及措施。

 III
RESEARCH ON THE MATURITY
EVALUATION OF EPC GENERAL
CONTRACTING PROJECT MANAGEMENT
-- TAKING THE PERSPECTIVE OF
ELECTRIC POWER DESIGN INSTITUTE
ABSTRACT
With the goal of " carbon peak " in 2030 and " carbon neutrality " in 2060,
China has begun to continuously promote the adjustment of industrial structure
and energy structure, accelerate the planning and construction of large-scale
wind power photovoltaic bases, and expand the scale of EPC general contracting
market for power construction projects. Previously, the electric power system
reform has continuously promoted the electric power design institute to face the
market with the opportunity of ' separation of main and auxiliary ', transform to
the EPC model, and give full play to the natural advantages of design as the
leading role in the overall planning of EPC general contracting project to open
up new markets. How to seek a stable balance point under the trend of
deepening the reform of the power mechanism and seize the opportunity of the
new energy policy to provide impetus for the growth of enterprises in the EPC
general
contracting
market,
and
to
continuously
enhance
the
market
competitiveness while accelerating the transformation is one of the difficulties
that the Electric Power Design Institute needs to break through. In this context,
this paper studies the evaluation model of EPC general contracting project
management maturity from the perspective of electric power design institute,
and puts forward countermeasures and suggestions to improve its project
management ability. The main research contents and conclusions are as follows :
(1) Based on the grounded theory and questionnaire survey method, the
evaluation index system of EPC general contracting project management
maturity is constructed from the perspective of electric power design institute,
including 18 elements and 56 indicators under the three classification
 IV
dimensions of EPC project management organization, EPC project management
elements and EPC project management process.
(2) The COWA-DEMATEL combination method is used to weight the
indicators. It not only considers the adverse effects of the comprehensive
independence and correlation weights on the calculation results, but also
analyzes the interaction between the indicators. The three-dimensional maturity
model OPM3 is selected as the basic model, and the traditional matter-element
extension model is improved by using the principle of asymmetric closeness
instead of the principle of maximum membership degree, so as to improve the
accuracy of the model on the basis of ensuring the comprehensiveness of
maturity evaluation.
(3) Taking the EPC general contracting project of the new project of YHP
coal-electricity integrated power plant as an example, the maturity evaluation
level is defined, and there is a slight trend to repeat the development level. The
evaluation results are consistent with the actual situation. CSA-BP neural
network is used to predict the evaluation results. On the one hand, it verifies the
feasibility of the maturity evaluation model built in this paper. On the other hand,
it provides a theoretical reference for dynamically predicting the maturity level
of EPC general contracting project management.
(4) Combined with the results of the comprehensive index weight and
maturity evaluation model, it is found that the electric power design institute is
not yet mature in the EPC general contracting project management, and then
gives suggestions and measures from the aspects of improving risk prevention
and control ability, improving communication and coordination mechanism and
procurement management mechanism, paying attention to financial management
and construction management, paying attention to the progress of the project,
strengthening the construction of EPC functions, changing the traditional design
concept, and improving service awareness.
