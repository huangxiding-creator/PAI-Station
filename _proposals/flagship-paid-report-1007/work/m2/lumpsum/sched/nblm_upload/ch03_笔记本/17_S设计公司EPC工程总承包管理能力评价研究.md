 摘 要

S设计公司EPC工程总承包管理能力评价研究 
学位类型：专业学位 
学科专业：工程管理（MEM） 
摘 要 
随着我国国民经济的高质量发展，建筑行业固定资产投资比重在缓慢下降。设计
企业的市场经营压力日益增加，近年来迅速发展的EPC 工程总承包业务为设计企业
持续健康发展提供了机会。S 设计公司为一家以工程设计、监理为主要业务的综合性
工程服务公司，在设计和监理业务持续下滑的市场背景下，正在大力拓展EPC 工程
总承包业务，但是目前S 设计公司的EPC 工程总承包管理能力无法满足项目管理的
需要。因此，通过科学客观的评价研究，找出影响S 设计公司EPC 总承包管理能力
的关键因素并提出有针对性的改进对策，具有重要的现实意义。 
本文主要研究过程如下：首先，介绍研究背景并提出本文的研究内容、方法和技
术路线。其次，对相关概念和理论进行阐述，为本文研究奠定理论基础。然后，介绍
S 设计公司EPC 工程总承包管理现状，采用问卷调查法找出S 设计公司EPC 工程总
承包管理存在的主要问题。接着，通过文献研究和问卷调查法对影响设计企业EPC 工
程总承包管理能力的关键因素进行识别和筛选，进而构建S 设计公司EPC 工程总承
包管理能力评价指标体系，并采用组合赋权法计算评价指标体系权重。随后，建立模
糊综合评价模型，对S 设计公司EPC 工程总承包管理能力进行评价，并对评价结果
进行分析找出影响S 设计公司EPC 工程总承包管理能力的关键因素。最后，根据评
价指标体系权重和评价结果提出改进S 设计公司EPC 工程总承包管理能力的对策。 
本文通过构建评价指标体系和评价模型，对S 设计公司EPC 工程总承包管理能
力进行了模糊综合评价，找出了影响S 设计公司EPC 工程总承包管理能力的关键因
素并提出了改进对策。研究结果为提升S 设计公司EPC 工程总承包管理能力奠定了
理论基础，也为同类设计企业提升EPC 工程总承包管理能力提供了一定的借鉴。 
关键词：设计公司；EPC 工程总承包；管理能力；模糊综合评价

ABSTRACT 
With the high-quality development of China's national economy, the proportion of fixed 
assets investment in the construction industry is slowly declining. Design enterprises are 
facing significant market survival pressure, and the rapidly developing EPC engineering 
general contracting business in recent years has provided opportunities for the sustainable 
and healthy development of design enterprises. S Company is a comprehensive engineering 
service company with engineering design and supervision as its main business. Against the 
backdrop of a continuous decline in design business, it is actively transitioning to EPC 
engineering general contracting business. However, the company's management capabilities 
cannot meet the needs of EPC engineering general contracting projects, which seriously 
affects the development of EPC engineering general contracting business. Therefore, it is 
necessary to identify the key factors that affect S Company's EPC general contracting 
management ability through quantitative research and develop targeted measures to enhance 
the EPC engineering general contracting management ability. 
The main research process of this article is as follows: Firstly, introduce the research 
background and propose the research content, methods, and technical roadmap of this article. 
Secondly, elaborate on relevant concepts and theories to lay the theoretical foundation for 
this study. Then, introduce the current situation of EPC engineering general contracting 
management in S Design Company, and use questionnaire survey method to identify the 
main problems in EPC engineering general contracting management in S Design Company. 
Subsequently, through literature research and questionnaire survey methods, key factors 
affecting the EPC engineering general contracting management capability of design 
enterprises were identified and screened, and an evaluation index system for the EPC

constructed. The weight of the evaluation index system was calculated using the combination

This article constructs an evaluation index system and evaluation model to conduct a 
fuzzy comprehensive evaluation of S Design Company's EPC engineering general 
contracting management ability, identifies key issues that affect S Design Company's EPC 
engineering general contracting management ability, and proposes improvement measures. 
The research results have laid the foundation for improving the EPC engineering general 
contracting management capability of S Design Company, and also have certain reference 
significance for evaluating the EPC engineering general contracting management capability 
of similar design enterprises.
