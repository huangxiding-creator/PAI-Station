 I
基于DEMATEL-ISM 法的铁路工程
EPC 总承包项目投资控制研究
摘要：随着EPC 总承包模式在铁路建设项目中的推广应用，针对铁
路工程EPC 总承包项目，如何进行有效的投资控制是一个重要的现
实问题。论文采用文献归纳、政策文本总结、案例分析以及DEMATEL
与ISM 相结合等方法，基于业主方角度，对铁路工程EPC 总承包项
目投资控制进行研究，主要研究工作及结论如下：
（1）分析了铁路工程EPC 总承包项目决策、设计、采购、施工、
竣工各阶段投资控制工作内容。
（2）运用文献分析、政策文本分析以及案例分析，从决策、设
计、采购、施工、竣工五个阶段识别出铁路工程EPC 总承包项目投
资控制的36 个影响因素；通过对专家问卷调查结果进行筛选与验证，
得到包括15 个影响因素的铁路工程EPC 总承包项目投资控制影响因
素清单。
（3）在分析并确定影响因素的重要程度以及层级结构基础上，
构建了铁路工程EPC 总承包项目投资控制影响因素5 层3 阶
DEMATEL-ISM 模型，根据各影响因素在铁路工程EPC 总承包项目
投资控制作用程度，将影响因素划分为表层因素、中间层因素和深层
因素。基于DEMATEL-ISM 模型，分析了各层级因素之间相互影响
关系以及对铁路工程EPC 总承包项目投资控制的影响。
（4）基于DEMATEL-ISM 模型分析结果，从投资控制的深层影
响因素、中间层影响因素以及表层影响因素三个层面，针对各层关键
影响因素提出了铁路工程EPC 总承包项目投资控制对策。
论文研究成果可为铁路工程EPC 总承包项目投资控制工作提供
现实参考。

Research on investment control of railway engineering EPC
projects based on DEMATEL-ISM method
Abstract：With the popularization and application of EPC mode in
railway construction projects, how to carry out effective investment
control is an important practical issue for railway engineering EPC
projects. The paper adopts the methods of literature induction, policy text
summary, case study and the combination of DEMATEL and ISM to
study the investment control of EPC EPC project of railway engineering
based on the owner's perspective, and the main research work and
conclusions are as follows:
(1)This paper analyzes the investment control work content in each
stage of decision making, design, procurement, construction and
completion of railway engineering EPC projects.
(2)This paper identifies 36 influencing factors of investment control
of EPC turnkey railway projects from five stages of decision making,
design, procurement, construction and completion through literature
analysis, policy text analysis and case study analysis. Through screening
and verification of expert questionnaire results, a list of influencing
factors of investment control of EPC turnkey railway projects including
15 influencing factors is obtained.
(3)Based on the analysis and determination of the importance degree
and hierarchical structure of the influencing factors, a 5-layer and 3-order
DEMATEL-ISM model of the influencing factors for investment control
of railway engineering EPC projects is constructed. According to the
degree of the role of each influencing factor in investment control of
railway engineering EPC projects, the influencing factors are divided into
surface factors, intermediate factors and deep factors . Based on the
DEMATEL-ISM model, the inter-influence relationship among the
factors at each level and the influence on the investment control of
railway engineering EPC general contracting projects are analyzed.
 IV
(4)Based on the analysis results of DEMATEL-ISM model, the
investment control countermeasures of railway engineering EPC projects
are proposed from the three levels of deep influence factors, intermediate
influence factors and surface influence factors for each level of
investment control.
The research results of the paper can provide a realistic reference for
the investment control work of railway engineering EPC projects.
Keywords: railway engineering; EPC general contracting project;
investment control; DEMATEL-ISM model
Classification: TU18
