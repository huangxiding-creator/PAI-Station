 摘要 
自2016 年住建部发布12 号文颁布以来，国内工程总承包市场的热度陡然上
涨，市场竞争激烈，总承包商企业规模逐渐壮大，但仍存在盈利能力不足、利润
率低迷等生存问题。鉴于业主选择实施工程总承包模式时，不同的合同计价模式
会导致总承包商面临风险程度以及利润空间各异。为应对日益复杂的项目管理风
险，总承包商亟需良好的合同风险管理能力，并且研究表明，适当合理的进行风
险防范，有助于总承包商的创收能力提升。为保证新发展阶段下总承包商鲜活生
命力，有必要深入探究工程总承包合同风险因素，因此，本文拟从总承包商角度，
对工程总承包总价合同风险进行以下研究：  
1.引入质量管理体系中的亲和图法对工程总承包合同风险因素进行识别。通
过文献梳理法、案例分析法和线上访谈法等质性研究方法，分析出项目中存在的
触发风险的事件，将可能对总承包商产生影响并承担后果的风险事件绘制成总承
包总价合同风险事件调查表，然后通过头脑风暴绘制亲和图，对工程总承包模式
下总承包商所承担的合同风险事件进行归类汇总：工程范围风险、发包人要求风
险、合同总价风险、合同条件风险、设计管理风险、现场条件风险、审计风险、
总承包商内部管理风险。 
2.基于投资偏差的工程总承包合同状态分析。基于合同状态理论，从投资目
标差异的方向构建工程总承包总价合同状态分析概念模型。从产生的风险传递链
条的设计、施工、竣工三个阶段出发，采用多案例研究方法从海量案例中甄别出
针对每个阶段的独特风险事件，然后将投资目标造成的工程量和合同价格的偏差
影响作为风险后果。研究结果表明设计阶段风险偏向于合同条件风险、发包人要
求风险、设计管理风险以及工程范围风险；施工阶段偏向于现场条件风险、总承
包商内部管理风险、合同总价风险；竣工阶段偏向于审计风险。 
3.基于PDPC 的工程总承包合同风险防范与创收策略。基于三阶段因事件源
引起的合同风险分类，采用顺向思维的PDPC 法，提出引起工程变更的工程范围、
发包人要求、设计管理、现场条件风险防范；引起调整价格的合同总价、合同条
件、总承包商内部管理的风险防范以及引起索赔的审计风险防范，提出总承包商
的创收策略。 
综上所述，运用亲和图通过工程总承包总价合同风险因素的辨识，然后运用
多案例分析总承包项目全过程中合同状态变化差异，在此基础上结合计价模式，
运用PDPC 法有针对性的提出不同类型风险防范与创收策略，对拓展总承包商创
收思路具有实践价值。

Abstract 
 
Since the issuance of Document No. 12 by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-
Rural Development in 2016, the popularity of the domestic general project contracting 
market has risen sharply, the market competition has been fierce, and the scale of 
general contractors has gradually grown, but there are still survival problems such as 
insufficient profitability and low profit margins. In view of the fact that when the owner 
chooses to implement the general contracting model of the project, different contract 
pricing models will lead to different levels of risk and profit margins for the general 
contractor. In order to cope with the increasingly complex project management risks, 
general contractors urgently need good contract risk management capabilities, and 
studies have shown that proper and reasonable risk prevention will help the general 
contractors to improve their ability to generate revenue. In order to ensure the fresh 
vitality of the general contractor in the new development stage, it is necessary to deeply 
explore the risk factors of the general contract of the project. Therefore, this paper 
intends to conduct the following research on the risk of the general contract of the 
general contract from the perspective of the general contractor: 
1. Introduce the affinity diagram method in the quality management system to 
identify the risk factors of the general contracting contract. Through qualitative research 
methods such as literature review method, case analysis method and online interview 
method, the events that trigger risks in the project are analyzed, and the risk events that 
may affect the general contractor and bear the consequences are drawn into the total 
contract price Contract risk event survey form, and then draw an affinity diagram 
through brainstorming to classify and summarize the contract risk events undertaken by 
the general contractor under the general project contracting mode: project scope risk, 
contractor requirement risk, total contract price risk, contract risk Condition risk, design 
management risk, site condition risk, audit risk, general contractor internal management 
risk. 
2. Status analysis of general contracting contract based on investment deviation. 
Based on the contract state theory, a conceptual model of the state analysis of the 
general contracting price contract is constructed from the direction of the difference of 
investment objectives. Starting from the three stages of design, construction and 
completion of the resulting risk transfer chain, the multi-case study method is used to 
identify the unique risk events for each stage from a large number of cases, and then 
 the deviation of the amount of work and the contract price caused by the investment 
target is calculated. Impact as a consequence of risk. The research results show that the 
risk in the design stage is biased towards the risk of contract conditions, the risk of the 
developer's requirements, the risk of design management and the risk of the project 
scope; the risk in the construction stage is biased towards the risk of site conditions, the 
internal management risk of the general contractor and the risk of the total contract 
price; the stage of completion is biased towards auditing risk. 
3. Risk prevention and revenue-generating strategy of general contracting contract 
based on PDPC. Based on the three-stage classification of contract risks caused by event 
sources, the PDPC method of forward thinking is used to propose the project scope, 
contractor requirements, design management, and site condition risk prevention that 
cause engineering changes; the total contract price and contract conditions that cause 
price adjustments , the risk prevention of the general contractor's internal management 
and the audit risk prevention of claims, and put forward the general contractor's 
revenue-generating strategy. 
To sum up, use the affinity diagram to identify the risk factors of the general 
contracting price contract of the project, and then use multiple cases to analyze the 
difference of the contract status in the whole process of the general contracting project.
