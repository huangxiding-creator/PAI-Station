质量保证金返还问题之研究 
——以《建设工程施工合同司法解释（一）》第17
条为中心 
（摘要） 
 
专   业：民商法学（全日制） 
               研究方向：不区分研究方向 
                   作者姓名：周庆园 
                   指导教师：王浩 
 
 
工程质量保证金乃发包人与承包人在建设工程施工协议中达成合意，在应该
支付的工程款中克扣部分，用来督促承包人在工程质量保证金返还期限内对建设
工程出现的缺陷予以保修的资金。首先在第一章中厘清工程质量保证金的内涵与
性质，然后从效果论的角度出发，试图弄清工程质量保证金是否能被优先受偿、
返还范围和比例，以及和违约金关系等问题。厘清工程质量保证金之性质，判断
是否能被优先受偿，笔者结合目前学界一些观点认为工程质量保证金其性质已经
发生转换，不同于普通的工程款，视情况或为信托所有权让与，或为金钱质押。
虽然工程质量保证金形式上与定金类似，定金根据法律规定可以抵作价款，但究
其本质而言，工程质量保证金其性质俨然发生转换，不同于普通工程款，除非当
事人另有规定可以优先受偿。当工程存在缺陷损失范围大于工程质量保证金，其
返还范围有自身特殊规则，不足部分可以主张赔偿，其不同于“违约金”处置规
则——以“抵销”为原则，“司法酌情增减”为例外。工程质量保证金与违约金
存在性质、目的、功能上的冲突，质保金和违约金只能选择适用，不能并用。 
然后从工程质量保证金返还之要件进行研究，其返还需要满足两个要件：一
为期限届满，一为质量合格。首先，就期限要件而言，《建设工程质量保证金管
理办法》（以下简称“质保金管理办法”）第2 条规定，工程质量保证金返还期
限最长不得超过2 年，《建设工程施工合同司法解释（一）》第17 条规定，当
 1 
事人可约定该期限之长短，并未局限于2 年。上位法与下位法冲突，司法实践中
同样存在相矛盾之判决：有的法院认为保证金返还期限长于2 年之约定，或者有
违公平原则，或者有违《质保金管理办法》的规定而无效；但有的法院认为超出
2 年的返还期限之约定，并无不妥，应遵从当事人的意思。故期限而言，笔者实
属困惑，工程质量保证金返还期限是否局限于2 年？其次，就质量要件而言，当
工程质量出现缺陷时，工程质量保证金直接抵扣和修复请求权优先哪种补正履行
方式更为合理？等等。笔者将在要件论中予以研究和探讨，笔者试图从法律位阶
的适用顺序、法律解释、意思表示解释等角度予以阐述，工程质量保证金的返还
期限，从通过完工验收之日起开始计算，如果因为发包人的原因未能及时验收而
擅自使用同样可发生验收之效果，期限届满仍需要返还工程质量保证金，其返还
期限不应一律局限于2 年之规定。就质量要件而言，我国补正履行方式无顺位要
求，采取合理选择模式，笔者认为瑕疵修补请求权权为先的补正履行方式更为合
理。

Author: Zhou Qingyuan

Project quality deposit refers to the fund agreed by the employer and the 
contractor in the construction contract to ensure the contractor to repair the defects of 
the construction project within the period of return of project quality deposit. The 
connotation of project quality margin return is two requirements: one is the expiration 
of the term, the other is qualified. Article 2 of the Measures for the Management of 
Construction Project Quality Earnest money stipulates that the maximum period for 
the return of construction project quality earnest money shall not exceed 2 years, and 
Article 17 of the Judicial Interpretation of Construction Project Construction Contract 
(I) stipulates that the parties may agree on the length of the period, not limited to 2 
years. There are conflicts between the upper law and the lower law, and there are 
contradictory judgments in judicial practice: some courts think that the time limit for 
the return of project quality deposit is deducted, or violates the principle of fairness, 
or violates the provisions of the "Measures for the Management of Construction 
Project Quality Deposit" and is invalid; However, some courts hold that the period 
beyond 2 years is in line with the true intentions of both parties and is effective 
according to the principle of good faith. Therefore, as for the term, the author is really 
confused. Is the term of project quality deposit return limited to 2 years? In terms of

deduction of engineering quality deposit and the right of repair claim are preferred, 
which is more reasonable? And so on. The author will be studied and discussed in the

law, such as intention explanation Angle is discussed, the engineering quality margin 
return period, from the start counting from the date of acceptance of completion, if the 
reason should be the developer fails to timely acceptance and unauthorized use of the 
same can happen effect of acceptance, The project quality deposit shall still be 
returned upon expiration of the term, and the term of return shall not be limited to 2 
years. As far as quality requirements are concerned, China's remedial performance 
mode has no sequential requirement and adopts reasonable selection mode. The 
author thinks that the remedial performance mode with defect repair claim first is 
more reasonable. 
Then, from the perspective of effect theory, the author tries to find out whether 
the project quality deposit can be paid first, the scope and proportion of the return, 
and the relationship between the project quality deposit and liquidated damages. To 
clarify the nature of the project quality bond and determine whether it can be 
compensated in a priority way, the author considers that the nature of the project 
quality bond has changed, which is different from ordinary project money. Depending 
on the situation, it is either the transfer of trust ownership or money pledge. Although 
the form of the project quality deposit is similar to the deposit, the deposit can be used 
as the price according to the law, but in essence, the nature of the project quality 
deposit is like a transformation, different from ordinary project funds, unless the 
parties have other provisions can be priority compensation. When the defect loss 
scope of the project is greater than the project quality deposit, its return scope has its 
own special rules, and the insufficient part can be claimed for insufficient 
compensation, which is different from the "liquidated damages" disposal rule, which

exception. Project quality deposit and liquidated damages are in conflict in nature,
