建设工程合同无效的认定及法律后果分析 
摘    要 
由于建设工程关系公众生命、财产安全，建设工程合同关系涉及
的法律、法规、规章等庞杂繁多，其中大量规定属于强制性规定。现
实中，由于建筑市场竞争激烈，在工程报批、招标投标、工程合同签
订和履行等环节的违法行为屡禁不止，如违法建设、串标围标、无资
质承包、转包、挂靠、违法分包等，由此引发大量关于合同效力的争
议，以致在司法实务中如何判别各种违法行为对合同效力的影响成为
建设工程合同纠纷不可回避的问题。 
合同效力一般包括有效、无效、效力待定、可撤销四种类型，而
建设工程合同效力主要为有效和无效两种类型。根据相关法律规定及
司法解释精神，要认定合同无效，首先须认定违法行为属于违反法律、
行政法规的强制性规定的行为；然后，须进一步认定该强制性法律规
定属于效力性强制性规定；另外，还须结合国际惯例和现实情况，对
相关法律规定的合理性进行必要的反思。本文将对施工合同纠纷中常
见违法行为对工程合同效力的影响进行分析探讨。 
建设工程施工合同通常具有标的额大，建设周期长、合同履行情
况复杂等特点，工程合同一旦被认定无效，将直接影响当事人的合同
预期及最终责任承担，特别是在工程造价、工期索赔、质量索赔、欠
款利息计算、相关行政责任等方面可能造成重大影响。因此，认定工
程合同无效仅解决了问题的一半，本文将对建设工程施工合同认定无
效后所产生的相关法律后果作进一步的探讨。 
 
关键词：建设工程合同  强制性规定  合同无效  法律后果

Determination of Invalid Building Construction 
Contract and Analysis of the Legal Consequence 
ABSTRACT 
As the building project relates to the safety of life and property of 
public people, construction contract involves many kinds of laws, and 
administrative regulations, and there are many mandatory norms therein. 
In fact, as the intensive competition in construction market, in the 
process of project approval, tendering and bidding, contract signing and 
fulfilling, there are many acts against the law, such as illegal construction, 
collusion in tendering and bidding, contracting without relevant 
qualification, contract out to third party, illegal subcontract, etc. the 
dispute for contract effect are related to or involve with these acts. And in 
legal practice, how to judge the effect upon the contract is unavoidable.  
In general, contract effect status including four types, namely valid, 
invalid, uncertain, and can be canceled. But the effect status for building 
project construction contract mainly valid and invalid. In case the contract 
is invalid, the illegal act(s) shall breach the mandatory norm(s) of law or 
administrative regulations firstly, and then the mandatory norm(s) shall 
influence the effect of contract directly. In addition, the rationality of the 
current mandatory norms also shall be re-considered combined with the 
international practice and status. This dissertation will discuss the effect of 
contract under the familiar illegal acts.  
The building project construction contract usually has the characters 
of big object sum, long construction period, and complicated contract 
performance. Once the contract is confirmed invalid, the contract party’s 
anticipation and final liability will be influenced directly, especially the 
serious consequence on final contract price, claim related to construction 
period or quality, the interest calculation, and the relevant administrative 
liabilities. Therefore, confirming the contract is invalid in only one part of
