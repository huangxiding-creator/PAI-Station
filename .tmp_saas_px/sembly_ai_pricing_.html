The free trial covers 1 meeting hour. Credits are allocated per user each month. MAX requires a minimum of 3 users; Enterprise starts at 10.

What about security on my plan?

Run any plan on the same security foundation.

Certified compliance Sembly holds SOC 2 Type II certification and operates in line with GDPR. HIPAA compliance is available on Max and Enterprise.
Model training control Every plan manages model training through opt-out settings, so your audio, video, and text stay excluded from training on your terms.
Verified trust Security documentation, subprocessor lists, and audit reports live in the Sembly Trust Center for your review before purchase.
What do I need to know before I choose?

Answer the last questions between you and a plan.

Basic fits one practitioner managing up to 25 clients from a single seat. Pro fits teams up to 40 that produce branded deliverables across a roster of up to 100 clients. Max fits organizations from 3 to 500 users that run high deliverable volume and need audit trails, SSO, and HIPAA.

Credits meter the AI work Sembly performs for you. Each user receives a monthly allowance: 2,500 on Basic, 3,000 on Pro, and 3,500 on Max. Every new workspace also starts with a one-time bonus of 2,500 credits. Unused credits expire at month end on Basic and Pro.

A client is a dedicated space inside your workspace that holds one customer's context, meetings, and knowledge. Each client carries its own branding, so every deliverable you generate for that customer arrives in their visual identity.

An AI artifact is a finished, branded deliverable Sembly generates from your accumulated knowledge: a presentation, report, case study, or sales pitch. Artifacts export as .pptx and .docx files ready to send under your name.

Yes. Start with our Free Trial plan to experience slide deck generation, meeting notes, and other features. Upgrade to the plan that fits your needs best when ready. Still have questions? Reach out to our support team for plan recommendations: support@sembly.ai

Annual billing lowers the monthly rate on every self-serve plan: Basic drops from $17 to $10, Pro from $29 to $20, and Max from $39 to $30 per user. You pay for the year upfront at the lower rate.

Basic covers exactly one user. Pro scales from 1 to 40 users. MAX starts at 3 users and scales to 500. Enterprise starts at 10 users with no upper limit.
