ChatGPT

openai.com/chatgpt

ChatGPT helps calculate odds, simulate outcomes, and analyze historical game logs. Players use it to identify winning patterns and structure bankroll goals. It's best for turning messy session notes into clear probability-based strategies.

Claude

anthropic.com/claude

Claude can summarize long spreadsheets of results or casino terms in plain language. You can feed it RTP sheets, variance data, or bonus terms and get a risk profile back. Ideal for filtering through dense PDFs quickly.

Perplexity

perplexity.ai

Use Perplexity to verify RTP values or confirm licensing data. It pulls results with citations, so you can double-check casino claims before depositing. It's good for quick factual lookups and license validation.

Wolfram|Alpha

wolframalpha.com

When you need precise numbers, Wolfram handles EV calculations and probability modeling. You can compute the likelihood of specific streaks or expected drawdowns on slots, giving you real math behind each risk.

Microsoft Excel with Copilot

microsoft.com/microsoft-365/excel

Excel with AI assistance is perfect for tracking bankroll over time. Ask it to chart streaks, visualize profit curves, and test staking systems. You'll see when volatility spikes before it drains your balance.

Google Colab

colab.research.google.com

Colab runs simulations right in your browser. Test 100k spin scenarios or create slot volatility heatmaps. A great free option for Python-based modeling without setup hassle.

Jupyter Notebook

jupyter.org

Build your own gambling lab locally. Combine pandas, NumPy, and matplotlib to measure expected value and variance for each slot or roulette system. It's a structured approach for long-term testing.

Notion AI

notion.so/product/ai

Notion AI keeps your gambling journal organized. It summarizes sessions, detects bad habits like chasing losses, and helps you stick to bankroll rules. Simplicity with structure.

Airtable AI

airtable.com/ai

Upload your play sessions and query them in natural language: "Which days had my highest volatility?" Airtable AI turns gambling data into insights without code.

Kaggle

kaggle.com

Use Kaggle's notebooks to test statistical models or neural nets on slot data. Simulate probability patterns and confirm or debunk "hot" slot myths with real math.

Use Cases: Analyzing Casino Documents with AI

Use AI tools to read fine print smarter than the house. Here's how:

  • Terms & Conditions: extract bonus rollover, country restrictions, and withdrawal caps with Claude or ChatGPT.
  • License Check: ask Perplexity to confirm license validity with official registries.
  • Bonus Modeling: compute true expected value after wagering multipliers.
  • Game Sheets: parse RTP/volatility PDFs in Colab to project drawdowns.
  • KYC Delays: summarize ID requirements and withdrawal timeframes to avoid holds.

How to use these tools effectively

  • Track everything: spin count, RTP, bankroll, and bonuses.
  • Simulate first: test scenarios before risking real money.
  • Set limits: fix stop-loss and win goals, and stick to them.
  • Verify info: confirm data from trusted sources before using it.

Your only real edge is discipline—tracking, testing, and quitting when the math says so.
