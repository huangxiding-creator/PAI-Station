What Is an AI Meeting Assistant

An AI meeting assistant is a software application that uses artificial intelligence to join, record, transcribe, and analyze meetings. Its primary purpose is to automate administrative tasks—specifically note-taking and task tracking—so human participants can focus on the discussion rather than the documentation.

It has three core pillars:

  • Capture: It “listens” to the audio via a virtual bot or plug-in to create a verbatim transcript
  • Synthesis: It uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to condense hours of talk into bulleted summaries and key takeaways
  • Action: It identifies specific commitments and deadlines, automatically turning them into “Action Items” or syncing them with project management tools

What To Look For in AI Meeting Assistants for Small Businesses

Unlike enterprise teams with dedicated IT departments, small businesses need tools that work out of the box. That’s because the wrong software creates more admin work than reducing it.

Evaluate these specific criteria before choosing a tool to simplify your meeting workflows:

  • Transcription accuracy and speaker identification: Can it reliably tell who said what during fast-paced calls with multiple speakers?
  • AI meeting summary quality: Does it produce concise recaps instead of walls of text you still have to parse?
  • Action item extraction: Does it detect commitments and deadlines rather than just highlighting keywords?
  • Integrations with your existing stack: Does it integrate natively with your project management and communication tools?
  • Privacy and data handling: Where is your meeting audio stored, and is it used to train AI models?
  • Ease of setup for non-technical teams: Can your team start using it today without an onboarding call?

Krisp best features
