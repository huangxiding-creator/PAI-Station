柏克德(中国)工程有限公司

ABOUT BECHTEL
Founded in 1898, Bechtel is one of the world's premier engineering, construction, and project management companies. Our 52,000 employees are currently teamed with customers, partners, and suppliers on a wide range of projects in 46 countries.
Bechtel has completed more than 22,000 projects in 140 countries including Hoover Dam, the Channel Tunnel, Hong Kong International Airport, the San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART) system, the reconstruction of Kuwait's oil fields after the Gulf War, Jubail industrial city, and CSPC Nanhai Petro Chemical Project.
www.bechtel.com
柏克德始创于1898年，是一家全球领先的美国工程建设及项目管理（EPC）公司，经与客户、合作伙伴、供应商通力合作，分布在全球46个国家的52,000名柏克德员工已完成了140多个国家的22,000多个工程项目，其中包括著名的胡佛大坝、英吉利海峡隧道、香港国际机场、旧金山湾区轨道交通系统、海湾战争后科威特油田的重建，沙特Jubail工业城及中海壳牌南海石化项目。
更多信息请登陆：http://www.bechtel.com
BECHTEL IN CHINA 柏克德在中国
Bechtel has been active in mainland China since 1979,completing more than 100 projects of all sizes, ranging from the $18 million Plantronics headset manufacturing facility to the $20 billion Hong Kong International Airport program – on schedule, within budget, and up to our clients’ and Bechtel high quality standards.
柏克德自1979年进入中国，在过去的三十几年里，已完成了从1800万美元的工厂设施到200多亿美元的香港国际机场等100多个项目，每个项目均在预算内以客户及柏克德最高质量标准如期完成。
Bechtel (China) Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd (BCEC)based in Shanghai with over 500 employees and also withpermanent offices in Beijing, Shenzhen,　Tianjin, Yantai and Taipei. BCEC is a wholly owned foreign enterprise providing full-service of engineering, procurement, construction, and project management in Greater China, as well as focusing on execution of large projects undertaken by Bechtel globally.
总部位于上海的柏克德 (中国 )工程有限公司，是柏克德在中国的全资子公司，上海办公室拥有超过500名员工，且公司在北京、深圳、天津、烟台及台北有分支机构。主要业务为面向大中国地区提供工程设计、采购、建造、项目管理等服务项目并积极参与柏克德在全球的大型项目。
To enhance the skills of our engineers, we have sentmore than 170 people to the US and other countries for on-the-job training and we expect many more high potential engineers will have the opportunity for offshore assignments in the coming years.
为提升员工的技术能力，我们已经向美国及世界其他地区派遣了超过170人次进行在岗培训，今后几年我们期望更多有潜力的工程师有机会到国外项目中进行岗位培训及工作。
In 2013, we are expanding our Shanghai Office’s capabilities to meet the requirements for fast developing business activities, support domestic projects in China and support various Bechtel projects worldwide.Please don’t hesitate to send us your résumé and become a part of the Bechtel Family.
为满足业务快速发展需求，承接、支持各类国内外工程建设项目，2013年，我们将继续扩充上海办公室工作人员，所以别再犹豫，赶快投递简历成为快速发展的柏克德大家庭中的一员吧！
