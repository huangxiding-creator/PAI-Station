Refer is further made to our letter 123, wherein stating the unforeseen event being arising in the course of the 
delivery of various Goods imported by the Contractor for your kind attention:

2. We exerted our efforts to ascertain the possibility and found that no alternative corridor for

The latest news release obtained from newspaper & media (website: http://reporter.akipress.org/news:20098/) 
are attached herewith as proof for your reference. Unfortunately, the  Customs house dedicated at the 
KARAMYK seems to be unlikely allowing any passage for the Lorries in transition. The Event has not 
ceased to the end and the ripple effects are being imposed on our performance of mobilization to some extent. 
 
According to Sub-Clause 19.1 Definition of Force Majeure —“in this Clause, Force Majeure means an 
exceptional event or circumstances:  
a) Which is beyond a Party’s control, 
b) Which such Party could not reasonably have provided against before entering into the Contract, 
c) Which, having arisen, such Party could not reasonably have avoided or overcome, and  
d) Which is not substantially attributable to the other Party.”  
 
We understand that the Event as mentioned-above shall be defined as Force Majeure and the Notice 
submitted through our letter 123 shall be deemed as a Notice given according to 19.2 Notice of Force 
Majeure because our obligation of mobilization(delivery of materials/plant) is being prevented till date. 
Therefore, we hereby submit you the Notice of Claim according to Sub-Clause 20.1, which is arising from 
the Sub-Clause 19.4 Consequences of Force Majeure. Meanwhile, we shall at all times use all reasonable 
endeavours to minimize any delay in the performance of the Contract as a result of such Force Majeure and 
timely give you notice when it ceases to be affected by the Force Majeure according to Sub-Clause 19.3 Duty 
to Minimize Delay.  
 
Your comments given through your letter 865 have been duly noted and we assure you to submit the 
contemporary records and particulars for substantiation in due course/time as per Sub-Clause 20.1.
