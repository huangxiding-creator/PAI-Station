# Risks of “Design, Procurement and Construction(EPC)/Turnkey Contract Conditions” and Countermeasures

> 来源: OpenAlex | 年: 2013 | 期刊: Advanced materials research | 被引: 0
> 查询: international engineering procurement construction turnkey

China's international project contracting business has developed rapidly, gained the success that attract worldwide attention. However, in recent years many enterprises met continuous losses. It is found that engineering contract losses are currently widely used international EPC contract mode. Compared with the conditions of construction contract of EPC mode and the traditional mode, risk allocation principles are very different. Analysis from the characteristics of EPC conditions of contract, focuses on the risk identification and prevention of the contractor, for the reference of Chinese engineering contracting business.
