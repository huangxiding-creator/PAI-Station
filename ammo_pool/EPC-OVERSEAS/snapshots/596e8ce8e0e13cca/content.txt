## Abstract

In construction contracts and FIDIC contracts, the engineer undertakes many activities within the framework of his specialization, scope of experience and skills, and he is subject to many obligations. When any error occurs, whether intentional or because of his negligence or failure to perform his job duties, many responsibilities arise on his shoulders.Whether civil in its two types, contractual or tortious, or moral responsibility, or criminal responsibility that arises if the engineer commits an act that constitutes a criminal offense, such as the collapse of the building he constructed, resulting in the death of a person.These responsibilities undoubtedly represent a burden on the engineer, and therefore he must be familiar with them and fully comprehend their details to avoid their occurrence. This is what the FIDIC Contract and the Civil Code attach great importance to, and specifically regulate, all aspects of FIDIC contracts.
