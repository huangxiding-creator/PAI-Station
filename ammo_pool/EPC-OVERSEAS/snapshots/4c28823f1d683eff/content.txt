of LOA with the following key scenarios: 
1. The Employer has provided us the new template for Performance Security on the Kick-off meeting

the Employer focusing on whether the assignment of the Bond might be amended with some 
additional conditions so as to mitigate the unknown risks. (Ref. 1&2) 
2. The confirmation of the template for PB was given by the Engineer on 1 st August 2012 after many 
mutual communications by mails (Ref 3~7). We noted that the final version of the template is 
consistent with the specimen given on the Kick-off meeting. It is also worth emphasizing that the 
item9 in the specimen “this Guarantee shall be governed by and construed in accordance with 
the laws of the Tajikistan. The courts of Tajikistan shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any 
dispute arising from or relating to this Guarantee” is totally inconsistent with the example form of 
Performance Security annexed in the RFP, wherein stating that “this Bond shall be governed by 
and construed in accordance with the law of Qatar. ” Please refer to your letter Ref No. 
QDDP-IMS-SNH-120817 -647 dated on 21 st August 2012 and you also duly noted such revision 
against the original one. Meanwhile, according to the Minutes of Kick-off meeting 3.2 “Such Bond 
from a Bank based in Qatar, e.g. the Bank which issued the Tender Bond will be considered 
acceptable to the Employer ”, hence, we recommended the Arab Bank Doha Plc. Qatari Branch 
which is the issuing bank for TB to issue the PB to the Employer. The Bank has been accepted by 
the Employer through the email sent by Cadas Evren Bayrak, PMP dated on 1 st August 2012 at 
22:20:47 Time.

At that juncture, we have no choice but to pursue the alternative Bank immediately. Definitely, such 
Event would be unforeseen by any experienced Contractor. According to the definition 1.1.6.8 
“unforeseeable”—means not reasonably foreseeable by an experienced Contractor by the date 
for submission of the Tender”, nobody would foresee that the Bank which already issued the TB at 
the earlier stage rejected to issue the PB for the same project. The reason of rejection is somehow 
attributable to the revision of Guarantee related to the applicable laws to be governed. Apparently, 
such problem/blame should not be solely apportioned to the Contractor.  
9. Subsequently, we again recommended the ICBC Doha Branch for the Employer ’s evaluation and 
the same has been conditionally accepted by the Employer through the meeting held on 12 th 
September 2012 in QD ’s Headquarter. Thereafter, the duly signed and stamped PB has been 
submitted on 19th September 2012 and accordingly, the handing over of the Site has taken place on 
21st September.        
 
In view of above-elaborated scenarios, we would like to highlight the followings for your kind attention: 
we understand that the timeframe –within 28days from the date of the LOA should be abided 
accordingly, but once any excusable delay event which is not only part of the default of the 
Contractor occurred, the Engineer should consequentially look into the same with due diligence and 
reasonably determine the extended time for submission of such documents to the extent that the 
finalization between the Contractor and the Employer could be foreseen. As long as the Employer has 
not yet received & accepted both the Documents, the NTP for the Project shall not be issued. 
Unfortunately, the Engineer has issued the NTP on 6th August 2012 determining the Commencement 
Date as 15 th August regardless of the conditions precedent to Commencement under the Contract, 
which is not acceptable to the Contractor.  
 
Under such circumstance, we hereby propose to alternatively amend the NTP as follows: 
l 20th September 2012 shall be defined as the date to commence the Works as per the clause 3 
of LOA and Sub-Clause 8.1 of GCC, by when the Employer has received and conditionally 
approved both the Documents. Subsequently, the Engineer has handed over the Site to the 
Contractor on 21st September 2012. 
 
Hopefully, the matter in this regard could be dealt with high priority by the Employer/Engineer and the 
agreement could be amicably reached in due course and in due time.
