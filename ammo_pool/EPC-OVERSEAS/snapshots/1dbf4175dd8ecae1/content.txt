## Abstract

FIDIC is the representative of the most independent consulting engineers and the most authoritative consulting engineers organization in the world.It promotes high-quality,high-level development of engineering consulting services worldwide.FIDIC contract is a crucial legal document for international building enterprise specially in civil engineering projects.This paper,taking EPC/Turnkey Contract Conditions as example,discusses its translation principles,language features,translation strategies and differences between two versions.Suggestions are put forward according to the problems caused by the misinterpretations during the process of domestic construction.
