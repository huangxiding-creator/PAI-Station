第一章

开工与动员 Mobilization

1. We received the Letter of Acceptance on 3 March.

我们于3月3日收到了中标函（接受函）。

场景：合同启动阶段，确认LOA日期，该日期是计算开工日期和工期的关键节点。

2. The Commencement Date is deemed to be 14 days after the Letter of Acceptance.

开工日期视为中标函发出后14天。

场景：核实合同约定的开工日期计算方式（常见于FIDIC条款14天默认规则）。

3. We are mobilizing site office, camp and temporary facilities this week.

我们本周正在进行现场办公室、营地和临建设施的动员工作。

场景：向业主/工程师报告动员进度。

4. The performance security has been submitted in the form of a bank guarantee.

履约担保已以银行保函形式提交。

场景：合同生效前提交履约保函，需确认金额、有效期、格式是否符合合同要求。

5. Please confirm receipt of the advance payment guarantee.

请确认已收到预付款保函。

场景：预付款支付前，承包商需先提交预付款保函。

6. The advance payment will be released within 21 days after receiving the guarantee.

预付款将在收到保函后21天内支付。

场景：说明预付款支付的合同时限。

7. We have appointed Mr. Li as the Contractor's Representative on site.

我们已任命李先生为现场承包商代表。

场景：正式书面通知业主/工程师指定代表人选。

8. The Engineer has approved our proposed project organization chart.

工程师已批准我们提交的项目组织架构图。

场景：动员阶段提交管理体系文件待批。

9. Access to the site was handed over on 10 March in accordance with Sub-Clause 2.1.

根据第2.1款，现场已于3月10日移交。

场景：确认现场移交（Right of Access to the Site）时间，直接影响工期起算。

10. Part of the site has not yet been handed over, which constitutes late possession of site.

部分现场尚未移交，构成现场延迟移交。

场景：现场移交不完整时，为后续工期索赔（EOT）留存证据。

11. We request the Engineer to confirm the site boundaries and reference points.

我们要求工程师确认现场边界和基准点。

场景：进场初期核实测量基准。

12. The baseline survey has been jointly conducted and signed off by both parties.

基线测量已由双方联合进行并签字确认。

场景：开工前地形/基准数据的联合确认，避免后期争议。

13. We are setting up the batching plant and workshop area as per the approved layout.

我们正按照已批准的总平面布置图设置搅拌站和加工车间。

场景：临时设施布置需先获批。

14. Temporary power and water supply have been connected to the site.

临时水电已接入现场。

场景：动员进度汇报。

15. The site access road is not yet complete, which is delaying equipment mobilization.

现场通道尚未完工，正在延误设备进场。

场景：说明外部因素影响动员进度，为潜在延误索赔铺垫。

16. Please issue the Notice to Proceed for the site clearance works.

请签发场地清理工作的开工令。

场景：某些分部工程需业主单独下达开工指令。

17. We have submitted the insurance policies as required under Sub-Clause 18.

我们已按第18款要求提交了保险单。

场景：提交工程一切险、第三者责任险等保单。

18. The insurance coverage will commence from the site handover date.

保险责任期自现场移交之日起生效。

场景：确认保险起保时间。

19. Kindly confirm the list of key personnel has been accepted.

请确认关键人员名单已获接受。

场景：项目经理、总工等关键岗位需业主/工程师批准。

20. We would like to replace the proposed Site Agent due to personal reasons.

因个人原因，我们希望更换拟任现场代理人。

场景：关键人员变更需书面申请并获批准。

21. The mobilization period is scheduled for 30 days from the Commencement Date.

动员期计划为开工日期后30天。

场景：明确动员期限，用于判断是否延误。

22. All statutory permits and licenses for site operation have been obtained.

现场施工所需的所有法定许可证均已取得。

场景：确认合规手续齐备。

23. We are still awaiting the import permit for the construction equipment.

我们仍在等待施工设备的进口许可证。

场景：说明外部审批延误对动员的影响。

24. The site security and fencing works have been completed as per HSE requirements.

现场安保及围挡工作已按HSE要求完成。

场景：现场安全前置条件确认。

25. Please find attached the mobilization schedule for your review.

请查收附件中的动员计划表以供审阅。

场景：正式提交文件时的常用措辞。

26. The joint site inspection will be held tomorrow at 9:00 am to verify existing conditions.

联合现场勘查将于明日上午9点举行，以核实现场现状。

场景：开工前联合勘查，记录原始地貌/既有设施状况，避免后期责任不清。

27. Please note any existing defects or damages should be recorded before we take possession.

请注意，在我们接管现场之前，任何既有缺陷或损坏都应予以记录。

场景：保护承包商权益，避免被误认为施工造成的损坏。

28. The temporary site office will be located adjacent to the main gate.

临时现场办公室将设置在主大门附近。

场景：现场布置说明。

29. We confirm that mobilization is now complete and works can commence as planned.

我们确认动员工作现已完成，工程可按计划开始。

场景：正式书面确认动员完成节点。

30. The Contractor's programme has been submitted within 28 days as required by Sub-Clause 8.3.

承包商的进度计划已按第8.3款要求在28天内提交。

场景：初始施工总进度计划的提交时限。

31. We request an extension of the mobilization period due to force majeure.

由于不可抗力，我们请求延长动员期。

场景：动员期延误的正式申请。

32. The kick-off meeting is scheduled for next Monday to align on project procedures.

启动会议定于下周一举行，以统一项目程序。

场景：项目正式开工前的内部/外部协调会。

33. Please provide the site-specific safety induction for all incoming personnel.

请为所有进场人员提供现场专项安全入场培训。

场景：新进场人员必须完成安全教育。

34. The Contractor shall establish a Quality Management Plan prior to commencement.

承包商应在开工前建立质量管理计划。

场景：合同要求提交的管理体系文件之一。

35. We have set up the laboratory on site for material testing purposes.

我们已在现场设立实验室用于材料检测。

场景：动员阶段配套设施建设。

36. The environmental management plan has been submitted for the Engineer's approval.

环境管理计划已提交给工程师审批。

场景：环保合规文件提交。

37. Site clearance and demolition works are progressing as per schedule.

场地清理及拆除工作正按计划推进。

场景：动员期工作进展汇报。

38. We are coordinating with local authorities regarding utility relocation.

我们正在与当地主管部门协调管线迁改事宜。

场景：常见的外部协调事项，也是常见延误诱因。

39. The existing underground utilities were not shown accurately on the reference drawings.

现有地下管线在参考图纸上标示不准确。

场景：地下障碍物问题，属于典型的不可预见障碍索赔情形（Sub-Clause 4.12）。

40. Please be advised that mobilization costs have exceeded the budget due to site access issues.

敬请注意，由于现场通道问题，动员成本已超出预算。

场景：动员阶段费用增加的正式通知，为后续索赔铺垫。

第二章

现场例会与沟通 Site Meetings

41. Let's go through the minutes of the last meeting before we start.

在开始之前，我们先过一遍上次会议纪要。

42. Item 3 remains open; please provide an update.

第3项仍未关闭，请提供最新进展。

43. This item is closed and will be removed from the outstanding list.

此项已关闭，将从待办清单中移除。

44. We have circulated the draft minutes for your comments within 3 days.

我们已发出会议纪要草稿，请在3天内提出意见。

45. If no comments are received, the minutes will be deemed accepted.

若未收到意见，纪要将被视为已获认可。

场景：常见的纪要确认机制表述，注意这不能替代正式的合同通知程序。

46. Please note this is a record of discussion, not a contractual instruction.

请注意，这仅为讨论记录，并非合同指令。

场景：会议中口头交流频繁，需明确区分"讨论"与具有合同效力的"指令"。

47. Any instruction given verbally will be confirmed in writing within 7 days.

任何口头指令将在7天内以书面形式确认。

场景：对应FIDIC第3.3款关于工程师指令需书面确认的原则。

48. Could you clarify whether this is a formal instruction under the Contract?

请问这是否为合同项下的正式指令？

场景：会议中及时厘清指令性质，避免后续争议。

49. We would like this recorded as a Contractor's concern, not an agreement.

我们希望将此记录为承包商的关切事项，而非双方一致意见。

50. Let's move on to the progress review section.

我们进入进度审查环节。

51. Overall progress this week is 2% behind the baseline programme.

本周整体进度比基线计划滞后2%。

52. The critical path activities are currently on schedule.

关键路径上的工作目前按计划进行。

53. We foresee a potential delay to the concrete pouring of Block C.

我们预见C区混凝土浇筑可能出现延误。

54. What is the root cause of the delay in rebar fabrication?

钢筋加工延误的根本原因是什么？

55. The delay is attributed to late delivery of imported materials.

该延误归因于进口材料交付延迟。

56. We request the Employer's confirmation on the outstanding design information.

我们请求业主对尚未确定的设计信息予以确认。

57. This issue has been outstanding for three consecutive meetings.

该问题已连续三次会议未能解决。

58. Can we set an action-by date for this item?

我们能否为此事项设定完成截止日期？

59. Action: Contractor to submit revised programme by Friday.

行动事项：承包商应于周五前提交修订后的进度计划。

60. Action: Engineer to respond to RFI-045 within 5 working days.

行动事项：工程师应在5个工作日内回复RFI-045。

61. We would like to raise a new agenda item regarding site access constraints.

我们希望新增一项议程，讨论现场通道限制问题。

62. Let's park this discussion and continue offline.

我们先暂缓此议题，会后另行沟通。

63. This matter is beyond the scope of this meeting and should be addressed separately.

此事项超出本次会议范围，应另行处理。

64. We disagree with the Engineer's interpretation and reserve our rights.

我们不认同工程师的解读，并保留我们的权利。

场景：会议中出现分歧时的标准表态，避免被视为默认接受。

65. Our position remains as stated in our letter dated 12 May.

我们的立场仍如5月12日函件中所述。

66. We note the Employer's comments but do not agree with the conclusion.

我们注意到业主的意见，但不同意其结论。

67. Could you please repeat that for the minutes?

请您为了记录再重复一遍好吗？

68. Let the record show that the Contractor raised this concern on 10 June.

请记录在案，承包商已于6月10日提出此关切。

69. We would like this statement to be reflected verbatim in the minutes.

我们希望此陈述能逐字记录在纪要中。

70. This is a joint decision and both parties agree to proceed accordingly.

这是双方共同决定，双方同意据此推进。

71. Please distribute the attendance list along with the minutes.

请将签到表与会议纪要一并分发。

72. The next progress meeting is scheduled for the same time next week.

下次进度会议定于下周同一时间召开。

73. We would like to request a special meeting to discuss the variation order.

我们希望申请召开专题会议讨论变更指令事宜。

74. Let's break into technical sub-groups to resolve the interface issues.

我们分成技术小组来解决接口问题。

75. The interface between civil and MEP works needs to be clarified.

土建与机电工程之间的接口需要进一步明确。

76. Who is the responsible party for coordinating this interface?

谁是负责协调此接口的责任方？

77. We suggest establishing a weekly coordination meeting between subcontractors.

我们建议在各分包商之间建立每周协调会机制。

78. Please ensure all relevant department heads attend the next meeting.

请确保各相关部门负责人出席下次会议。

79. The Employer's representative was absent, so this item could not be resolved.

由于业主代表缺席，此事项未能得到解决。

80. We would appreciate written confirmation of what was agreed today.

希望能以书面形式确认今天达成的一致意见。

81. This agreement is subject to written confirmation from head office.

此协议须经总部书面确认后方可生效。

82. Please hold your questions until the end of the presentation.

请在汇报结束后再提问。

83. Let's prioritize the top three risks for discussion today.

我们今天优先讨论排名前三的风险。

84. The safety statistics for this month show zero lost-time incidents.

本月安全统计显示零工时损失事故。

85. We would like to commend the site team for maintaining good housekeeping standards.

我们对现场团队保持良好的文明施工标准表示赞赏。

86. Any other business before we close the meeting?

在结束会议前还有其他事项吗？

87. Thank you all for attending; the meeting is now closed.

感谢各位的出席，会议现在结束。

88. We would like to schedule a site walk before the next meeting.

我们希望在下次会议前安排一次现场巡查。

89. Please ensure the meeting room is booked with video conferencing facilities.

请确保会议室已预订并配备视频会议设施。

90. The consultant's comments on the shop drawings are still pending.

顾问对施工详图的意见仍在等待中。

91. We would like an update on the status of the long-lead items.

我们想了解长周期采购物项的最新状态。

92. This is a recurring issue and requires management-level attention.

这是一个反复出现的问题，需要管理层层面的关注。

93. We propose escalating this matter to senior management for resolution.

我们建议将此事上报高层管理进行解决。

94. Let's document the key decisions made in today's meeting.

我们把今天会议中做出的关键决策记录下来。

95. The minutes will serve as the official record unless disputed in writing.

除非以书面形式提出异议，本纪要将作为正式记录。

96. We reserve our right to comment further after internal review.

我们保留在内部审核后进一步提出意见的权利。

97. Please note this discussion does not constitute a waiver of our contractual rights.

请注意，本次讨论并不构成对我方合同权利的放弃。

98. We would like this meeting minuted as "under protest."

我们希望本次会议以"保留异议"的方式记录在案。

99. The technical query raised last week has now been resolved.

上周提出的技术问题现已解决。

100. Let's confirm the format and frequency of progress reporting going forward.

我们确认一下今后进度报告的格式和频率。

101. We would like clarification on who chairs the next coordination meeting.

我们想明确一下下次协调会议由谁主持。

102. This point was already discussed and agreed in the meeting on 5 April.

此事项已在4月5日会议上讨论并达成一致。

103. We note the discrepancy between today's discussion and the previous minutes.

我们注意到今天的讨论与此前纪要存在出入。

104. Could we get a written response instead of a verbal one on this matter?

关于此事，我们能否得到书面回复而非口头答复？

105. We would like the Engineer's written instruction to proceed with this work.

我们希望获得工程师的书面指令以推进此项工作。

106. All parties acknowledge the contents of these minutes as accurate.

各方确认本纪要内容准确无误。

107. Please forward the meeting minutes to all site engineers for reference.

请将会议纪要转发给所有现场工程师作参考。

108. We would like to table this issue for the monthly progress meeting instead.

我们希望将此事项提交至月度进度会议讨论。

109. The subcontractor's representative did not attend; please follow up separately.

分包商代表未出席，请另行跟进。

110. This concludes today's agenda; thank you for your cooperation.

今天的议程到此结束，感谢各位的配合。

第三章

图纸、技术澄清与RFI

111. Please issue RFI-032 regarding the reinforcement detail at Grid Line 5.

请就5号轴线的配筋详图问题签发RFI-032。

112. We require clarification on the discrepancy between the structural and architectural drawings.

我们需要就结构图与建筑图之间的不一致之处进行澄清。

113. This drawing has been superseded by Revision C issued on 20 April.

该图纸已被4月20日发布的C版修订替代。

114. Please confirm which revision governs for construction purposes.

请确认施工应以哪个版本为准。

115. The IFC (Issued for Construction) drawings have not yet been received for this section.

该分区的施工图（IFC）尚未收到。

116. We cannot proceed with this work until the technical query is resolved.

在该技术问题得到解决之前，我们无法开展此项工作。

117. Kindly expedite your response as this is holding up work on the critical path.

恳请尽快回复，因该问题正影响关键路径工作的推进。

118. The response to RFI-018 was received 12 days after submission, exceeding the agreed turnaround time.

RFI-018的回复在提交后12天才收到，超出了约定的回复时限。

场景：为后续工期延误索赔积累证据，需记录RFI回复超期天数。

119. We attach a marked-up drawing showing our proposed solution for your review.

我们附上标注的图纸，展示我们提议的解决方案，请审阅。

120. Please advise if this constitutes a design change requiring formal instruction.

请告知这是否构成需要正式指令的设计变更。

121. The specification is silent on this particular detail; please provide guidance.

规范对该具体细节未作说明，请予以指导。

122. There is a conflict between Section 3 of the Specification and the drawings.

规范第3节与图纸之间存在冲突。

123. In case of conflict, please confirm the order of precedence of contract documents.

如有冲突，请确认合同文件的优先适用顺序。

124. We propose an alternative method of construction; please review the attached technical submission.

我们提议采用替代施工方法，请审阅附件中的技术方案。

125. This RFI is submitted for information only and does not require formal response.

本RFI仅供参考，无需正式回复。

126. Please note that RFI responses marked "See Drawing" without further detail are insufficient.

请注意，仅标注"见图纸"而无进一步说明的RFI回复是不充分的。

127. We request a site instruction to formalize the verbal clarification given yesterday.

我们要求签发现场指令，以正式确认昨日给出的口头澄清。

128. The as-built dimensions differ from the design drawings; please advise on the way forward.

实测尺寸与设计图纸不符，请就下一步处理方式给出指导。

129. Please confirm the load-bearing capacity assumption used in the design.

请确认设计中所采用的承载力假设值。

130. We have identified a potential clash between the ductwork and the structural beam.

我们发现风管与结构梁之间存在潜在碰撞。

131. A coordination drawing (BIM clash detection) has been issued to resolve the conflict.

已发布协调图（BIM碰撞检测）以解决该冲突。

132. The Engineer's comment "Approved as Noted" requires the following items to be addressed.

工程师"备注通过"的批复意见要求处理以下事项。

133. Please resubmit the shop drawing incorporating the Engineer's comments.

请根据工程师意见修改后重新提交施工详图。

134. The submittal has been rejected; please revise and resubmit within 7 days.

该报审文件已被退回，请在7天内修改并重新提交。

135. We consider this comment to be a change in scope beyond the original design intent.

我们认为该意见构成超出原设计意图的范围变更。

136. Please clarify whether this is a "Comment," an "Instruction," or an "Approval."

请明确此意见属于"评论"、"指令"还是"批准"。
