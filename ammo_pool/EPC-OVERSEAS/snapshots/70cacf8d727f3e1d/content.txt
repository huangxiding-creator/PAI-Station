## Abstract

“ Bad China ” has become a general theme of journalistic and academic commentary on contemporary China. This book adopts framing analysis of English-language media in China and Australia and transcription of interviews with “ intermediate elites ” in Australia to evaluate to what extent there is congruence between domestic and overseas accounts of Chinese governance
