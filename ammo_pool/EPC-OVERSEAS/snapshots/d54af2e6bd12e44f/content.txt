## Abstract

The in-depth promotion of the Belt and Road Initiative provides a historic opportunity for oil cooperation and development between China and the Middle East. This paper systematically examines the legal environment characteristics of major resource-rich countries in the Middle East, deeply analyzes the institutional, compliance and remedial risks faced by China's petroleum cooperation, and evaluates the effectiveness of the existing multiple protection mechanisms. The study finds that the legal environment of Middle East oil cooperation shows deep differences on a common basis, and there is a structural imbalance between "hard law" and "soft law" in the current protection mechanism. Based on this, this paper proposes to construct a dynamic adaptive protection framework that runs through the whole investment process, integrates treaty empowerment, contract foundation building, and relationship stability maintenance, and puts forward differentiated strategies and institutional innovation suggestions for different countries. This study is helpful to enrich the legal guarantee theory of energy cooperation along the Belt and Road and provide a decision-making reference for Chinese enterprises to improve the risk prevention and control capabilities of Middle East oil cooperation and for government departments to improve the legal support system for overseas energy cooperation.
