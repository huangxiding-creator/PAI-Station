辩论背景｜Debate Backgrounds

1. English Debate

Background

Allegations of corruption arise with increasing frequency in investor-State arbitration and are often directed at the circumstances in which an investment was originally obtained. When raised as jurisdictional objections, such allegations may dispose of an investor’s claim at the threshold, before the tribunal considers the merits of the dispute. Where a tribunal instead proceeds to the merits, the same allegations may be relied upon again -- under the “clean hands” principle, illegality doctrines, or other legal grounds -- and may defeat the claim in whole or in part. Tribunals have reached divergent conclusions on materially similar facts, and the issue remains unsettled. This debate is framed by the following illustrative scenario.

A foreign investor obtains the right to build and operate a power plant in the host State under a long-term concession. In the course of securing that concession, its representatives make payments to officials of the host State. The project is subsequently financed, constructed, and placed into operation. Several years later, the host State terminates the concession, takes over the power plant, and refuses to pay compensation, maintaining that the concession had been procured through bribery. The investor commences arbitration under the applicable bilateral investment treaty, alleging unlawful expropriation. The host State contends that an investment founded upon a concession procured through bribery is not entitled to protection under the treaty.

The Motion

Where an investment opportunity was procured through bribery but the investment was subsequently made and implemented, the resulting investment should be denied the protection of the treaty in its entirety.

2.中文辩论

背景

建设工程仲裁中的工期、质量及造价争议，通常涉及延误分析、缺陷认定及工程估值等专业问题， 仲裁庭可能需要专家协助。实践中，专家证据主要通过两种方式提出：其一，由当事人各自委任专家，分别提交报告，并参加专家会商、交叉询问等；其二，由仲裁庭在听取双方意见后指定一名或多名专家，就特定事项提交报告，由双方提供资料、发表意见并进行询问。在国际工程仲裁 中，前一种方式较为常见；在中国仲裁实践中，则后一种方式较为常见。

本场辩论基于以下情形展开：

中国甲公司（业主）与中国乙公司（EPC 总承包商）因某境外大型基础设施项目发生争议， 依约提交某中国仲裁机构仲裁，仲裁地为中国。争议涉及工期延误、工程质量及变更估值； 仲裁庭由三名法律专业人士组成，认为需要工期、技术和造价专家协助。双方均能获取项 目资料并聘请合格专家，未约定专家证据的组织方式，也尚未提交专家报告。适用的仲裁 规则允许采用上述两种模式，仲裁庭须选择其中一种作为本案的主要专家证据机制。无论 选择何种模式，专家报告及其所依据的资料均应向双方披露，双方均享有陈述、质证和询问专家的权利。专家意见不拘束仲裁庭，也不取代仲裁庭作出最终判断。

辩题

在中国企业之间的境外工程仲裁中，当工期、质量及造价等专业问题需要专家协助时，应当以当事人各自委任专家作为主要专家证据机制，而非由仲裁庭指定专家。
