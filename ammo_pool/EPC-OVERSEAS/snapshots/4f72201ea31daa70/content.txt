# Strategies of “Go Globe”for Chinese EPC

> 来源: OpenAlex | 年: 2015 | 期刊: Ke-ji guanli yanjiu | 被引: 0
> 查询: Chinese contractors overseas EPC projects risk

In recent years,a growing number of Chinese contractors have participated in international competition through the mode of EPC. A scientific analysis and an accurate positioning of strategies for the development of international EPC projects are very important for Chinese contractors to succeed in venturing overseas. Eight key obstructing factors of go globefor Chinese EPC are extracted based on empirical studies. From the following three aspects: strengthening basic operational capacity,enhancing the capabilities of integrated management and risk response for contractors,improving the external environment,the paper proposes a strategy to provide useful reference for the decision- making of Chinese engineering companies to participate in international EPC contracting business.
