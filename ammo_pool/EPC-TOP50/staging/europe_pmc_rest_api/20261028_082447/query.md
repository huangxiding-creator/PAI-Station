原文: https://europepmc.org/article/MED/42811581

**摘要/描述摘录:**
> The repair of chronic wounds is a complex medical challenge involving multiple factors. The diagnosis, treatment, and nursing process are intricate and challenging, imposing a heavy burden on both patients and healthcare providers. With the advancement of clinical medicine, new technologies and methods for local wound treatment are constantly emerging. The wound management principles provide an evidence-based framework for medical staff to standardize clinical practice. The MOIST (M: moisture balance, O: oxygen balance, I: infection control, S: support strategy, T: tissue management) concept was proposed for the first time by a German expert group in 2017 and was updated by an interdisciplinary expert group in 2023, aiming to provide standardized guidance for the treatment and care of chronic wounds. Currently, this concept has been widely applied internationally and has both educational and clinical practice guidance functions. Based on the MOIST concept and focusing on the essential issues in chronic wound management, this article systematically elaborates on the key points, difficulties, and latest progress, with the aim of providing an evidence-based reference for clinical practice.

## [Application and challenges of the clinical decision support system in hematological diseases].

*2026 | 10.3760/cma.j.cn121090-20251012-00460*

原文: https://europepmc.org/article/MED/42409744

**摘要/描述摘录:**
> Hematological diseases, particularly hematological neoplasms, are highly heterogeneous, characterized by diverse clinical manifestations and complex biological features. Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) is an auxiliary diagnosis and treatment system that integrates clinical data, medical expertise, and artificial intelligence (AI), which is expected to significantly improve the standardization, accuracy, and efficiency of diagnosis and treatment for hematological diseases and thereby improve prognosis. This review aimed to systematically summarize the application status of CDSS in hematological diseases, discuss its core value, analyze the current challenges, and outline future development. CDSS has potential in standardized diagnosis and treatment, accurate diagnosis and treatment, early prevention, and efficiency improvement of hematological diseases, including hematological neoplasms and bleeding and coagulation disorders. However, it faces many challenges, such as data processing, knowledge base construction and maintenance, model performance optimization, system integration, and a lack of evaluation criteria. In the future, CDSS is expected to integrate multicenter and multimodal data to enable precise, individualized, and whole-process hematological disease management.
