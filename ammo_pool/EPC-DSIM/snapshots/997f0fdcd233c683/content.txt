我国基础建设水平在世界领先，随着我国与“一带一路”国家的深化合作，越来越多建工企业“走出去”承包公路、桥梁、港口、炼油厂等大型海外工程。我国企业在开拓海外市场过程中，由于对项目地形数据不足、对海外市场不熟悉、对国际工程法律问题不了解、国际形势复杂多变等原因，在项目实施过程中遇到过很多问题。笔者曾协助不同承包商处理多项国际工程索赔，以下将结合经验对承包国际工程项目不同阶段的风险因素和应对措施进行分析，本文分为三部分，此为上篇，包括投标报价阶段和合同磋商阶段的风险及应对措施。
China's infrastructure is the most advanced in the world. As the cooperation between China and the 'Belt and Road' countries continues to intensify, an increasing number of construction enterprises are expanding their operations to include large-scale projects in foreign countries, including refineries, ports, highways, and bridges. Chinese enterprises have encountered numerous challenges during project implementation as a result of the complex and volatile international situation, unfamiliarity with overseas markets, insufficient terrain data of the project site, and a lack of understanding of international engineering legal issues. The authors have provided support to a variety of contractors in the management of numerous international engineering claims. Based on result of our experience, the following analysis will focus on the factors of risks and countermeasures at various stages of contracting international engineering projects. The full article shall include three parts, the Part One of which is presented as following.
一、投标报价阶段的风险及应对措施
Risks and Countermeasures in the Bid Pricing Stage
在开拓海外市场、争取国际工程项目的过程中，前期投标阶段的风险管理显得尤为关键。为了急于获得项目合同，企业在投标前往往忽视了对招标文件的深入剖析和项目现场条件的充分调研，盲目追求低价策略，导致投标文件中出现遗漏项，报价策略缺乏科学性与合理性，为后续项目实施埋下了成本超支、利润压缩乃至合同纠纷的隐患。
Risk management is particularly critical during the early bidding stage when exploring overseas markets and securing international engineering projects. Companies frequently neglect to conduct a comprehensive examination of tender documents and conduct an adequate investigation of project site conditions prior to bidding in their eagerness to secure project contracts. This heedless pursuit of low-price strategies results in omissions in the bid documents and a dearth of scientific and rational pricing strategies. Consequently, this establishes the foundation for cost overruns, profit compression, and even contract disputes during project implementation.
（一）从实践案例看投标阶段的风险
Risk Analysis during the Bidding Stage from Practical Cases
1. 乌干达欧文水电站扩建项目低价竞争导致项目亏损
The Uganda Owen Falls Hydro Power Station Expansion Project suffered project losses as a result of low-price competition.
项目在1992年2月8日的投标共有来自全球的13家承包公司参与竞逐，其中欧洲承包商占据了多数席位。四川国际公司提交了总额为6387万美元的具有竞争力的报价。尽管在项目初期，监理公司、世界银行及项目业主方均对欧洲公司的投标表现出较强的偏好，但四川国际公司凭借其显著的价格优势，成功吸引了各方的关注与考量。经过多轮评审与深入协商，该项目最终于1993年11月5日顺利完成签约，项目于1994年1月正式动工。
The bidding for the project was conducted on February 8, 1992, with a total of 13 contracting companies from around the world participating. European contractors occupied the majority of seats. Competitive bids totaling $63.87 million were submitted by Sichuan International Company. Sichuan International Company successfully drew the attention and consideration of all parties due to its substantial price advantage, despite the initial preference of the supervision company, the World Bank, and the project owner for European companies' bids. The project contract was finally signed on November 5, 1993, and officially commenced in January 1994, following numerous rounds of reviews and in-depth negotiations.
然而，自1996年4月起，由于资金短缺问题的持续发酵，项目施工进展严重受阻，几乎陷入了全面停滞的状态。项目业主方于1996年9月7日终止了与四川国际公司的合同关系，直接导致其在该年度遭受了超过8300万元人民币的巨额亏损。此次事件不仅是对四川国际公司项目管理能力的一次严峻考验，也凸显了在国际工程承包中资金保障与风险控制的重要性。未来，国际工程承包商应总结此次四川国际公司经验，加强项目前期的资金规划与风险管理，以更加稳健和专业的姿态参与国际市场竞争
Nevertheless, the project's construction was significantly impeded, nearly halting, as a result of the persistent funding shortages that had existed since April 1996. The project owner terminated the contract with Sichuan International Company on September 7, 1996, as a direct consequence of which the company suffered a substantial loss of over 83 million RMB that year. Sichuan International Company's project management capabilities were not only severely tested by this incident, but the significance of financial security and risk control in international engineering contracting was also underscored. Contractor of international construction need to conduct a thorough examination of experience of Sichuan International Company, enhance the financial planning and risk management of projects during the early stages, and engage in international market competition with a more professional and stable stance in the future.
2. 非洲某跨国桥项目在投标阶段未对业主的合同陷阱充分识别
The owner's contract traps were not fully identified during the bidding stage of the Cross-border Bridge project in Africa.
以某国企在非洲某跨国桥项目为例，业主通过打乱或者调整合同文件优先次序，从合同中排除了对于不确定性很高的文件，从而规避了业主方责任。
Taking a state-owned enterprise's Cross-border Bridge project as an example, the owner disrupted or adjusted the priority order of contract documents, excluding highly uncertain documents from the contract, thereby avoiding the owner's responsibility.
（1）业主通过专有条款把地质研究报告排除在合同文件之外，从而排除地质勘查的相关责任。现实际施工中证实，招标文件提供的较近距离的土场填料质量均不满足规范要求，导致承包商后期施工中因选择较远土场而增加了较多的运费和土资源费成本。
The Employer excluded the geological survey report from the contract documents through specific clauses, thereby excluding related liabilities for geological surveys. However, it has been confirmed during actual construction that the quality of the filler in the closer distance provided in the bidding documents does not meet the regulatory requirements, resulting in the increase in freight and soil resource costs for our company due to the selection of a farther soil site in the later construction of the Contractor.
（2）在签订合同协议书时，业主把通用行政条款规定的合同文件解释顺序中投标函的附件（已标价工程部清单和单价定义）删除，导致已标价工程量清单和单价定义解释顺序在特别行政条款、一般行政条款、技术条款和图纸之后，在发生单价定义与技术规范和图纸冲突时，技术规范和图纸解释优先于单价定义。如主桥桩基钢护筒实例，承包商投标报价时根据单价定义中对护筒壁厚等于10mm的规定计算成本，但是技术规范对护筒壁厚的规定为最小厚度为10mm，施工中因地质原因导致护筒壁厚需增加至25mm，因合同协议书中技术规范解释顺序优先于单价定义，导致承包商无法在因为护筒壁厚增加需进行单价调整时处于不利地位。
When signing the contract agreement, the Employer omitted the attachments to the Letter of Tender (including the priced bill of quantities and unit price definitions) from the order of precedence for interpreting contract documents as stipulated in the General Administrative Provisions. Consequently, the priced bill of quantities and unit price definitions are now placed after the Special Administrative Provisions, General Administrative Provisions, Technical Provisions, and Drawings in the order of precedence. In cases where there are conflicts between unit price definitions and technical specifications or drawings, technical specifications and drawings take precedence over unit price definitions. For instance, in the case of steel casing for the main bridge pile foundation, the Contractor’s bidding quotation was based on the unit price definition which stipulated a casing wall thickness of 10mm. However, the technical specifications specified a minimum thickness of 10mm, and due to geological conditions during construction, the casing wall thickness needed to be increased to 25mm. Since the technical specifications in the contract agreement take precedence over unit price definitions, the Contractor is at a disadvantage when it comes to adjusting unit prices due to the increased casing wall thickness.
（3）承包商在投标阶段未提交同为合同文件的组成部分的单价分析表（单价和包干价），承包商在执行阶段时发现合同清单单价分布畸形（有些高至成本价1-2倍，有些低至成本价四分之一至三分一），导致在执行阶段无法计算并提交合理的单价分析表。
In the Bidding process, the Contractor did not submit a unit price analysis table (unit price and lump sum price), which is also an integral part of the contract documents. When it comes to the execution of the contract, the Contractor found that the distribution of the unit price of the contract list was abnormal (some as high as 1-2 times the cost price, some lower than 1/4 to 1/3 of the cost price), resulting in the inability to calculate and submit a reasonable unit price analysis table during the implementation stage.
（4）业主在专用条款中增加了修改引起的差额大于合同原始金额5%才适用调价公式的前提，提高了承包商调价的难度，导致承包商需要承担修改差额低于合同原始金额5%
