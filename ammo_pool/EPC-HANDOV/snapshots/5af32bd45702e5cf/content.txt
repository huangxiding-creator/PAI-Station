# 2020版建设项目工程总承包合同（中英文对照）

原创  2026-09-28 18:55 四川

2020版建设项目工程总承包合同

（中英文对照）

GENERAL CONTRACT FOR ENGINEERING PROCUREMENT CONSTRUCTION (EPC) PROJECT

1.1 词语定义和解释

1.1 Definitions and Interpretation

合同协议书、通用合同条件、专用合同条件中的下列词语应具有本款所赋予的含义：

The following terms in the Contract Agreement, the General Conditions of Contract and the Particular Conditions of Contract shall have the meanings given to them in this Article:

1.1.1.6 《发包人要求》：指构成合同文件组成部分的名为《发包人要求》的文件，其中列明工程的目的、范围、设计与其他技术标准和要求，以及合同双方当事人约定对其所作的修改或补充。

1.1.1.6 “Employer's Requirements” means the document entitled the Employer's Requirements, which forms part of the Contract Documents, specifying the purpose, scope, design and other technical standards and requirements for the Project, together with any amendments or supplements thereto agreed by the Parties.

1.1.1.7 项目清单：是指发包人提供的载明工程总承包项目勘察费（如果有）、设计费、建筑安装工程费、设备购置费、暂估价、暂列金额和双方约定的其他费用的名称和相应数量等内容的项目明细。

1.1.1.7 “Project Schedule” means the schedule provided by the Employer setting out the items and corresponding quantities in respect of the General Contracting Project, including survey fees (if any), design fees, construction and installation costs, equipment procurement costs, provisional sums, the provisional sum and the names and quantities of other fees agreed by the Parties.

1.1.1.8 价格清单：指构成合同文件组成部分的由承包人按发包人提供的项目清单规定的格式和要求填写并标明价格的清单。

1.1.1.8 “Price List” means the list completed by the Contractor showing the prices, in accordance with the format and requirements of the Project Schedule provided by the Employer, which forms part of the Contract Documents.

1.1.1.9 承包人建议书：指构成合同文件组成部分的名为承包人建议书的文件。承包人建议书由承包人随投标函一起提交。

1.1.1.9 “Contractor's Proposal” means the document entitled the Contractor's Proposal, which forms part of the Contract Documents and is submitted by the Contractor together with the Letter of Tender.

1.1.2.6 工程师：是指在专用合同条件中指明的，受发包人委托按照法律规定和发包人的授权进行合同履行管理、工程监督管理等工作的法人或其他组织；该法人或其他组织应雇用一名具有相应执业资格和职业能力的自然人作为工程师代表，并授予其根据本合同代表工程师行事的权利。

1.1.2.6 Engineer means the corporation or other organization specified in the Particular Conditions, commissioned by the Employer to carry out the work of contract performance management, engineering supervision and administration in accordance with the provisions of law and the Employer's authorization; such corporation or other organization shall employ a natural person with appropriate professional qualifications and competence as the Engineer's Representative and grant him the right to act on behalf of the Engineer under this Contract.

1.1.2.7 工程总承包项目经理：是指由承包人任命的，在承包人授权范围内负责合同履行的管理，且按照法律规定具有相应资格的项目负责人。

1.1.2.7 Project Manager means the project leader appointed by the Contractor, who has the relevant qualifications required by law and is authorized by the Contractor to be responsible for the management of the performance of the Contract.

1.1.4.3 开始现场施工日期：包括计划开始现场施工日期和实际开始现场施工日期。计划开始现场施工日期是指合同协议书约定的开始现场施工日期；实际开始现场施工日期是指工程师发出的符合法律规定的开工通知中载明的开始现场施工日期。

1.1.4.3 Commencement Date for Site Work：means the scheduled Commencement Date for Site Work and the actual Commencement Date for Site Work. The scheduled Commencement Date for Site Work is the start date stipulated in the Contract Agreement; the actual Commencement Date for Site Work is the date specified in the Commencement Notice for Site Work issued by the Engineer in accordance with applicable Laws.

1.1.4.4 竣工日期：包括计划竣工日期和实际竣工日期。计划竣工日期是指合同协议书约定的竣工日期；实际竣工日期按照第8.2款[竣工日期]的约定确定。

1.1.4.4 Completion Date：means the scheduled Completion Date and the actual Completion Date. The scheduled Completion Date is the date stipulated in the Contract Agreement; the actual Completion Date shall be determined in accordance with Sub-Clause 8.2 [Completion Date].

3.3 工程师

3.3 Engineer

3.3.1 发包人需对承包人的设计、采购、施工、服务等工作过程或过程节点实施监督管理的，有权委任工程师。工程师的名称、监督管理范围、内容和权限在专用合同条件中写明。根据国家相关法律法规规定，如本合同工程属于强制监理项目的，由工程师履行法定的监理相关职责，但发包人另行授权第三方进行监理的除外。

3.3.1 If the Employer deems it necessary to supervise and manage the Contractor's design, procurement, construction, services and other work processes or milestones, the Employer shall have the right to appoint an Engineer. The Engineer's name, scope, content and authority of supervision shall be stated in the Particular Conditions. Under and in compliance with the relevant State laws and regulations, if the Works under this Contract are subject to compulsory supervision, the Engineer shall perform and discharge the relevant duties of statutory supervision, unless otherwise authorized by the Employer to a third party for such supervision.

3.3.2 工程师按发包人委托的范围、内容、职权和权限，代表发包人对承包人实施监督管理。若承包人认为工程师行使的职权不在发包人委托的授权范围之内的，则其有权拒绝执行工程师的相关指示，同时应及时通知发包人，发包人书面确认工程师相关指示的，承包人应遵照执行。

3.3.2 The Engineer shall supervise and administer the Contractor on behalf of the Employer within the scope, content, duties and authorities entrusted by the Employer. If the Contractor considers that the authority exercised by the Engineer is not within the scope of authorization entrusted by the Employer, the Contractor shall have the right to refuse to carry out the relevant instructions of the Engineer and shall promptly notify the Employer. However, if the Employer confirms the relevant instructions of the Engineer in writing, the Contractor shall comply with such instructions.

3.6 商定或确定

3.6 Agreement or Determination

3.6.1 合同约定工程师应按照本款对任何事项进行商定或确定时，工程师应及时与合同当事人协商，尽量达成一致。工程师应将商定的结果以书面形式通知发包人和承包人，并由双方签署确认。

3.6.1 Where the Contract provides that the Engineer shall agree or determine any matter in accordance with this Sub-Clause, the Engineer shall negotiate with the Parties without undue delay in an endeavour to reach agreement. The Engineer shall notify the Employer and the Contractor in writing of the agreed result, and the Parties shall sign it to confirm.

3.6.2 除专用合同条件另有约定外，商定的期限应为工程师收到任何一方就商定事由发出的通知后42天内或工程师提出并经双方同意的其他期限。未能在该期限内达成一致的，由工程师按照合同约定审慎做出公正的确定。确定的期限应为商定的期限届满后42天内或工程师提出并经双方同意的其他期限。工程师应将确定的结果以书面形式通知发包人和承包人，并附详细依据。

3.6.2 Unless otherwise stated in the Particular Conditions, the period for agreement shall be 42 days after the Engineer receives the notice from either Party on the matter to be agreed, or such other period as proposed by the Engineer and agreed by both Parties. If no agreement is reached within such period, the Engineer shall, with due care, make a fair determination in accordance with the Contract. The period for determination shall be 42 days after the expiry of the period for agreement, or such other period as proposed by the Engineer and agreed by both Parties. The Engineer shall notify the Employer and the Contractor in writing of the determination, with detailed grounds attached.

第4条 承包人

Article 4 Contractor

4.1 承包人的一般义务

4.1 Contractor‘s General Obligations

除专用合同条件另有约定外，承包人在履行合同过程中应遵守法律和工程建设标准规范，并履行以下义务：

Unless otherwise agreed in the Particular Conditions, the Contractor shall, in performing the Contract, comply with applicable laws and construction standards and specifications, and perform the following obligations:

（1）办理法律规定和合同约定由承包人办理的许可和批准，将办理结果书面报送发包人留存，并承担因承包人违反法律或合同约定给发包人造成的任何费用和损失；

(1) To procure the licenses and approvals required of the Contractor by law and the Contract, submit the same in writing to the Employer for retention, and bear any costs, losses and damages caused to the Employer by the Contractors violation of law or the Contract;

（2）按合同约定完成全部工作并在缺陷责任期和保修期内承担缺陷保证责任和保修义务，对工作中的任何缺陷进行整改、完善和修补，使其满足合同约定的目的；

(2) To complete all the Works as stipulated in the Contract, assume liability for defects and provide warranties during the Defects Liability Period and the warranty period, and rectify, complete and repair any defects in the Works to meet the purposes intended by the Contract;

（3）提供合同约定的工程设备和承包人文件，以及为完成合同工作所需的劳务、材料、施工设备和其他物品，并按合同约定负责临时设施的设计、施工、运行、维护、管理和拆除；

(3) To provide the Plant, Contractor‘s Documents, and the labour, materials, construction equipment and other items required for the execution of the Works, and be responsible for the design, construction, operation, maintenance, management and demolition of temporary facilities as stipulated in the Contract;

4.7 承包人现场查勘

4.7 Contractor's Inspection of the Site

4.7.1 除专用合同条件另有约定外，承包人应对基于发包人提交的基础资料所做出的解释和推断负责，因基础资料存在错误、遗漏导致承包人解释或推断失实的，按照第2.3项[提供基础资料]的规定承担责任。承包人发现基础资料中存在明显错误或疏忽的，应及时书面通知发包人。

4.7.1 Unless otherwise provided in the Particular Conditions, the Contractor shall be responsible for the interpretations and inferences made on the basis of the basic data submitted by the Employer. If the Contractor's interpretations or inferences are inaccurate due to errors or omissions in such basic data, the Contractor's liability shall be determined in accordance with Sub-Clause 2.3 [Provision of Basic Data]. If the Contractor discovers any manifest errors or omissions in the basic data, it shall promptly notify the Employer in writing thereof.

4.9 工程质量管理

4.9 Project Quality Management

4.9.1 承包人应按合同约定的质量标准规范，建立有效的质量管理系统，确保设计、采购、加工制造、施工、竣工试验等各项工作的质量，并按照国家有关规定，通过质量保修责任书的形式约定保修范围、保修期限和保修责任。

4.9.1 The Contractor shall, in accordance with the quality standards and specifications stipulated in the Contract, establish an effective Quality Management System, and exercise due diligence to ensure the quality of the design, procurement, manufacturing, construction, completion testing and other works, and shall, pursuant to relevant national regulations, stipulate the warranty scope, warranty period and warranty liability in the form of a quality warranty commitment.

4.9.2 承包人按照第8.4款[项目进度计划]约定向工程师提交工程质量保证体系及措施文件，建立完善的质量检查制度，并提交相应的工程质量文件。对于发包人和工程师违反法律规定和合同约定的错误指示，承包人有权拒绝实施。

4.9.2 The Contractor shall, in accordance with Article 8.4 [Project Schedule], submit the Quality Assurance System and documents on its quality assurance measures to the Engineer, establish a sound quality inspection system, and submit the relevant project quality documents. The Contractor shall be entitled to refuse to implement any incorrect instructions from the Employer or the Engineer that violate the law or the provisions of the Contract.

5.1 承包人的设计义务

5.1 Contractor's Design Obligations

5.1.1 设计义务的一般要求

5.1.1 General Requirements for Design Obligations

承包人应当按照法律规定，国家、行业和地方的规范和标准，以及《发包人要求》和合同约定完成设计工作和设计相关的其他服务，并对工程的设计负责。承包人应根据工程实施的需要及时向发包人和工程师说明设计文件的意图，解释设计文件。

The Contractor shall complete the design work and other services related thereto in accordance with the law, national, industrial and local codes and standards, the Employer's Requirements, and the provisions of the Contract, and shall be responsible for the design of the Works. The Contractor shall, as necessary for the execution of the Works, explain the intent of and interpret the design documents to the Employer and the Engineer in a timely manner.

5.1.2 对设计人员的要求

5.1.2 Requirements for Designers

承包人应保证其或其设计分包人的设计资质在合同有效期内满足法律法规、行业标准或合同约定的相关要求，并指派符合法律法规、行业标准或合同约定的资质要求并具有从事设计所必需的经验与能力的的设计人员完成设计工作。承包人应保证其设计人员（包括分包人的设计人员）在合同期限内，都能按时参加发包人或工程师组织的工作会议。

The Contractor shall ensure that its own or its design subcontractors' design qualifications satisfy the relevant requirements of laws, regulations, industry standards or the Contract throughout the validity period of the Contract, and shall assign designers who meet the said qualification requirements and possess the necessary experience and capability to perform the design work. The Contractor shall ensure that its design staff (including the design staff of its subcontractors) are able to attend the working meetings organized by the Employer or the Engineer on time throughout the Contract Period.

6.2.3 材料和工程设备的保管

6.2.3 Storage of Materials and Plant

（1）发包人供应材料与工程设备的保管与使用

(1) Storage and Use of Materials and Plant Supplied by the Employer

发包人供应的材料和工程设备，承包人清点并接收后由承包人妥善保管，保管费用由承包人承担，但专用合同条件另有约定除外。因承包人原因发生丢失毁损的，由承包人负责赔偿。

发包人供应的材料和工程设备使用前，由承包人负责必要的检验，检验费用由发包人承担，不合格的不得使用。

The Materials and Plant supplied by the Employer shall, after being counted and received by the Contractor, be properly stored by the Contractor, and the cost of storage shall be borne by the Contractor, unless otherwise stipulated in the Particular Conditions. Any loss or damage caused by the Contractor's fault shall be compensated by the Contractor.

Prior to the use of the Materials and Plant supplied by the Employer, the Contractor shall be responsible for the necessary inspection, and the cost of such inspection shall be borne by the Employer. Unqualified items shall not be used.

（2）承包人采购材料与工程设备的保管与使用

(2) Storage and Use of Materials and Plant Procured by the Contractor

承包人采购的材料和工程设备由承包人妥善保管，保管费用由承包人承担。合同约定或法律规定材料和工程设备使用前必须进行检验或试验的，承包人应按工程师的指示进行检验或试验，检验或试验费用由承包人承担，不合格的不得使用。

工程师发现承包人使用不符合设计或有关标准要求的材料和工程设备时，有权要求承包人进行修复、拆除或重新采购，由此增加的费用和（或）延误的工期，由承包人承担。

The Materials and Plant procured by the Contractor shall be properly stored by the Contractor, and the cost of storage shall be borne by the Contractor. Where the Contract or laws provide that Materials and Plant must be inspected or tested prior to use, the Contractor shall carry out such inspection or testing in accordance with the Engineer's instructions, and the costs thereof shall be borne by the Contractor. Unqualified items shall not be used.

If the Engineer discovers that the Contractor is using Materials and Plant that do not meet the design or relevant standard requirements, the Engineer shall have the authority to require the Contractor to repair, dismantle or re-procure the same, and any additional costs and/or delay in the Construction Period incurred thereby shall be borne by the Contractor.

6.5.1 试验设备与试验人员

6.5.1 Test Equipment and Test Personnel

（1）承包人根据合同约定或工程师指示进行的现场材料试验，应由承包人提供试验场所、试验人员、试验设备以及其他必要的试验条件。工程师在必要时可以使用承包人提供的试验场所、试验设备以及其他试验条件，进行以工程质量检查为目的的材料复核试验，承包人应予以协助。

(1) The Contractor shall provide the testing site, testing personnel, testing equipment and other necessary testing conditions for the on-site material tests carried out by the Contractor under the Contract or as instructed by the Engineer. The Engineer may, if necessary, use the testing site, testing equipment and other testing conditions provided by the Contractor to carry out material verification tests for the purpose of checking the quality of the Works, and the Contractor shall render assistance thereto.

（2）承包人应按专用合同条件约定的试验内容、时间和地点提供试验设备、取样装置、试验场所和试验条件，并向工程师提交相应进场计划表。承包人配置的试验设备要符合相应试验规程的要求并经过具有资质的检测单位检测，且在正式使用该试验设备前，需要经过工程师与承包人共同校定。

(2) The Contractor shall provide the test equipment, sampling devices, testing site and testing conditions in accordance with the test contents, time and location stipulated in the Particular Conditions, and shall submit the corresponding mobilization schedule to the Engineer. The test equipment procured by the Contractor shall comply with the requirements of the relevant test procedures and shall be tested by a qualified testing institution, and shall be jointly calibrated by the Engineer and the Contractor prior to its formal use.

（3）承包人应向工程师提交试验人员的名单及其岗位、资格等证明资料，试验人员必须能够熟练进行相应的检测试验，承包人对试验人员的试验程序和试验结果的正确性负责。

8.1.2 开始工作通知

8.1.2 Notice of Commencement

经发包人同意后，工程师应提前7天向承包人发出经发包人签认的开始工作通知，工期自开始工作通知中载明的开始工作日期起算。

With the Employer’s consent, the Engineer shall give the Contractor a Notice of Commencement, countersigned by the Employer, at least 7 days in advance. The Time for Completion shall be calculated from the Commencement Date stated in such notice.

除专用合同条件另有约定外，因发包人原因造成实际开始现场施工日期迟于计划开始现场施工日期后第84天的，承包人有权提出价格调整要求，或者解除合同。发包人应当承担由此增加的费用和（或）延误的工期，并向承包人支付合理利润。

Unless otherwise stated in the Particular Conditions, if the actual date for commencement of Site works is delayed by more than 84 days after the planned commencement date for Site works due to a reason attributable to the Employer, the Contractor shall be entitled to claim an adjustment of the Contract Price, or to terminate the Contract. The Employer shall bear any additional costs incurred and/or delays to the Time for Completion caused thereby, and shall also pay the Contractor reasonable profit.

8.2 竣工日期

8.2 Completion Date

承包人应在合同协议书约定的工期内完成合同工作。除专用合同条件另有约定外，工程的竣工日期以第10.1条[竣工验收]的约定为准，并在工程接收证书中写明。

The Contractor shall complete the Contract Works within the time limit stated in the Contract Agreement. Unless otherwise stipulated in the Particular Conditions, the Completion Date of the Works shall be determined in accordance with Sub-Clause 10.1 [Taking-Over of Works] and shall be stated in the Acceptance Certificate for the Works.

因发包人原因，在工程师收到承包人竣工验收申请报告42天后未进行验收的，视为验收合格，实际竣工日期以提交竣工验收申请报告的日期为准，但发包人由于不可抗力不能进行验收的除外。

If, due to reasons attributable to the Employer, the Engineer fails to carry out the taking-over inspection within 42 days after receipt of the Contractor’s application report for completion acceptance, the Works shall be deemed to have passed such inspection, and the actual Completion Date shall be the date of submission of the said application report; provided that this shall not apply where the Employer is unable to carry out the inspection due to Force Majeure.

第14条 合同价格与支付

Article 14 Contract Price and Payment

14.1 合同价格形式

14.1 Contract Price Form

14.1.1 除专用合同条件中另有约定外，本合同为总价合同，除根据第13条[变更与调整]，以及合同中其它相关增减金额的约定进行调整外，合同价格不做调整。

14.1.1 Unless otherwise specified in the Particular Conditions, this Contract is a lump sum contract. Except as adjusted under Article 13 [Variations and Adjustments] or other provisions of the Contract providing for additions or deductions, the Contract Price shall not be adjusted.

14.1.2 除专用合同条件另有约定外：

14.1.2 Unless otherwise specified in the Particular Conditions:

(1) 工程款的支付应以合同协议书约定的签约合同价格为基础，按照合同约定进行调整；

(1) Payments shall be adjusted in accordance with the Contract, based on the Contract Price stated in the Contract Agreement;

(2) 承包人应支付根据法律规定或合同约定应由其支付的各项税费，除第13.7款[法律变化引起的调整]约定外，合同价格不应因任何这些税费进行调整；

(2) The Contractor shall pay all taxes, duties, charges and fees payable by it under the law or the Contract. Except as provided in Sub-Clause 13.7 [Adjustments for Changes in Legislation], the Contract Price shall not be adjusted for any such taxes, duties, charges and fees;

14.3.1 工程进度付款申请

14.3.1 Application for Progress Payment

（1）人工费的申请

(1) Application for Labour Costs

人工费应按月支付，工程师应在收到承包人人工费付款申请单以及相关资料后7天内完成审查并报送发包人，发包人应在收到后7天内完成审批并向承包人签发人工费支付证书，发包人应在人工费支付证书签发后7天内完成支付。已支付的人工费部分，发包人支付进度款时予以相应扣除。

The Labour Costs shall be paid on a monthly basis. The Engineer shall, within 7 days after receiving the Application for Payment of Labour Costs and relevant supporting documents, complete the review and submit it to the Employer. The Employer shall, within 7 days after receipt thereof, complete the examination and approval, and issue the payment certificate for Labour Costs to the Contractor. The Employer shall complete the payment within 7 days after the issuance of such payment certificate. The paid Labour Costs shall be deducted accordingly from the progress payment to be made by the Employer.
