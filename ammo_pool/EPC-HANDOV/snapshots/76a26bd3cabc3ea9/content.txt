# 风能发电场设计采购建造EPC工程总承包合同（中英文对照）

原创  2026-08-31 16:37 四川

ENGINEERING, PROCUREMENT, & CONSTRUCTION (EPC) AGREEMENT OF WIND FARM

风能发电场设计采购建造EPC工程

总承包合同 （中英文对照）

RECITALS

鉴于条款

(A) The Employer intends to develop a wind farm project (the "Project") at the location stated above;

（1）雇主拟在项目地点开发一个风电场项目（下称"项目"）；

(B) The Contractor has the requisite qualifications, expertise and experience in the engineering, procurement and construction of wind farms;

（2）承包商具备风电场工程、采购与施工所需的相应资质、专业知识与经验；

(C) The Employer wishes to engage the Contractor to carry out the Works on an EPC (single point responsibility) basis, and the Contractor has agreed to carry out and complete the Works on such basis in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

（3）雇主拟按 EPC（单点责任）模式委托承包商实施本工程，承包商同意依照本合同条款按该模式实施并完成本工程。

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

据此，双方约定如下：

ARTICLE 1 DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

第一条 定义与解释

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

1.1 除上下文另有要求外，下列术语在本合同中应具有下述含义：

"Wind Farm" means the wind farm to be constructed under this Agreement, comprising the Wind Turbine Generators, foundations, electrical works, collector circuits, substation (as applicable), SCADA system and associated balance of plant;

"风电场"系指依本合同拟建设之风电场，包括风电机组、基础、电气工程、集电线路、升压站（如适用）、SCADA 系统及相关配套工程；

"Wind Turbine Generator" or "WTG" means each wind turbine generator to be supplied and installed under this Agreement;

"风电机组"或"WTG"系指依本合同应供应并安装的每一台风力发电机组；

"Power Curve Warranty" means the warranted power curve set out in [the Technical Specification / Appendix ____];

"功率曲线保证"系指〔技术规范/附件____〕所载的保证功率曲线；

"Availability Warranty" means the warranted availability of the WTGs set out in [Appendix ____];

"可用率保证"系指〔附件____〕所载的风电机组保证可用率；

"Interface Schedule" means the schedule regulating the main points of interface between the Contractor and other contractors (if any) engaged by the Employer;

"接口计划"系指规范承包商与雇主另行聘用的其他承包商（如有）之间主要接口事项的计划；

"Grid Code" means the applicable grid connection code and technical requirements of the relevant grid operator;

"电网规范"系指相关电网运营者适用的并网规程与技术条件；

"O&M Agreement" means any operation and maintenance agreement (including any long term service agreement, service and warranty agreement or service and availability agreement) entered into in relation to the Wind Farm;

"运维协议（O&M 协议）"系指就风电场订立的任何运行与维护协议（包括任何长期服务协议、服务与质保协议或服务与可用率协议）；

ARTICLE 2 SCOPE OF WORK

第二条 工程范围

2.1 The Contractor shall, on an EPC single point responsibility basis, carry out and complete the design, engineering, procurement, manufacture, supply, construction, installation, testing, commissioning and taking over of the Works in accordance with this Agreement, the Technical Specification [Appendix ____] and the Employer's Requirements.

2.1 承包商应在 EPC 单点责任模式下，依照本合同、〔技术规范附件____〕及雇主的要求，实施并完成本工程的设计、工程、采购、制造、供应、施工、安装、试验、调试及移交。

2.2 The Works comprise, without limitation:

2.2 工程范围包括但不限于：

(a) the supply and installation of the WTGs and the SCADA System;

（1）风电机组及 SCADA 系统的供应与安装；

(b) the construction of the foundations for the WTGs and other miscellaneous civil works for the Wind Farm;

（2）风电机组基础的建设及风电场其他零星土建工程；

(c) the electrical works, including the cables from the WTGs to the substation and the construction, upgrade or extension of the onshore or offshore substation (as applicable); and

（3）电气工程，包括自风电机组至升压站的电缆，以及陆上或海上升压站（如适用）的新建、升级或扩建；及

(d) all other works necessary for the Wind Farm to achieve Take Over and to operate in accordance with the Contract.

（4）为使风电场实现移交并按合同运营所必需的全部其他工程。

ARTICLE 3 CONTRACT PRICE, PAYMENT AND TAX

第三条 合同价格、支付与税费

3.1 The Contract Price shall be a fixed lump sum price, inclusive of all costs, taxes, duties, fees and expenses necessary for the full and complete performance of the Works by the Contractor, and shall not be subject to adjustment except as expressly provided in this Agreement (including pursuant to Article 14 (Variations), Article 21 (Force Majeure) and the provisions on changes in Laws).

3.1 合同价格应为固定总价，包含承包商全面完整履行本工程所需的一切成本、税费、规费及开支，除本合同明确规定的调整外（包括依第 14 条（变更）、第 21 条（不可抗力）及法律变更相关条款），不得调整。

3.2 The Contract Price shall be paid by the Employer to the Contractor in accordance with the payment schedule set out in [Appendix ____] upon the achievement of the corresponding payment milestones. Payment shall be made by wire transfer in RMB (or such other currency as agreed) to the Contractor's designated bank account within ____ days after receipt of a valid tax invoice (fapiao) issued in accordance with the Laws.

3.2 雇主应按〔附件____〕所载的付款计划，在相应付款节点成就后向承包商支付合同价格。款项应在收到依法律开具的合法税务发票后____日内，以人民币（或双方约定的其他币种）电汇至承包商指定银行账户。

3.3 The Employer shall pay an advance payment of ____% of the Contract Price to the Contractor within ____ days after receipt of the advance payment security and the Contractor's invoice.

3.3 雇主应在收到预付款担保及承包商发票后____日内，向承包商支付合同价格____%的预付款。

3.4 ____% of each payment (other than the advance payment) shall be retained by the Employer as retention money and shall be released in accordance with [Appendix ____].

3.4 除预付款外，每笔付款的____%应由雇主留存作为保留金，并按〔附件____〕约定予以释放。

3.5 All taxes arising in connection with this Agreement shall be borne and paid in accordance with the Laws. The Employer shall be entitled to such tax invoices and withholding procedures as are required by the Laws.

3.5 与本合同相关的一切税费应依照法律承担并缴纳。雇主有权获得法律要求的相应发票及代扣代缴手续。

ARTICLE 4 CONTRACTOR'S OBLIGATIONS

第四条 承包商义务

4.1 The Contractor shall carry out and complete the Works with due care and diligence, in accordance with this Agreement and the applicable Laws, and in a manner consistent with internationally recognised standards of good practice for EPC contractors in the wind energy industry.

4.1 承包商应尽职尽责，依照本合同及适用法律，并按风能行业 EPC 承包商公认的国际良好实践标准实施并完成本工程

4.2 The Contractor shall provide all personnel, materials, plant, equipment, temporary works and everything else, whether of a temporary or permanent nature, required for the design, procurement, construction, installation, testing, commissioning and completion of the Works.

4.2 承包商应提供本工程设计、采购、施工、安装、试验、调试与竣工所需的全部人员、材料、施工机械、设备、临时工程及其他一切临时性或永久性事项。

4.3 The Contractor shall establish and maintain a quality management system and quality control procedures in accordance with the applicable Laws and the Technical Specification, and shall submit to the Employer the quality plans and inspection and testing procedures for the Works.

4.3 承包商应依照适用法律及技术规范建立并维持质量管理体系与质量控制程序，并应向雇主提交本工程的质量计划及检验试验程序。

ARTICLE 5 EMPLOYER'S OBLIGATIONS

第五条 雇主义务

5.1 The Employer shall provide the Contractor with access to and possession of the Site and shall provide the Employer's Information in accordance with Article 17.

5.1 雇主应依照第 17 条向承包商提供进入现场及占有现场的条件，并提供雇主资料。

5.2 The Employer shall be responsible for obtaining and maintaining all consents, permits and approvals required for the Project which are the responsibility of the Employer under the Laws or under this Agreement (including grid connection consents, land use approvals and planning approvals), and shall give the Contractor reasonable assistance in obtaining those consents and permits which are the responsibility of the Contractor.

5.2 雇主应负责取得并维持依法律或依本合同应由雇主负责的项目所需的一切同意、许可与批准（包括并网许可、用地批准及规划批准），并应就承包商负责取得的许可给予合理协助。

5.3 The Employer shall pay the Contract Price in accordance with Article 3.

5.3 雇主应依照第 3 条支付合同价格。

5.4 Without prejudice to Article 5.2, the Employer shall not be responsible for the Contractor's failure to achieve the requirements of the Works where such failure is caused by the Contractor's own acts or omissions.

5.4 在不影响第 5.2 条的前提下，因承包商自身作为或不作为导致其未能满足工程要求的，雇主不承担责任。

5.5 Installation Vessels (offshore projects): Where the Project is an offshore wind farm project, the Parties shall agree in [Appendix ____] the allocation of the risk in respect of the installation vessel(s), including: (i) the technical requirements for the installation vessel; (ii) the period for which the installation vessel is required and whether such period is a fixed or a shifting window; (iii) the apportionment of costs for having the installation vessel for a period longer than originally envisaged; and (iv) the remedies if the installation vessel does not function in accordance with the

specified requirements or is not available.

5.5 安装船舶（海上项目）：如项目为海上风电场项目，双方应在〔附件____〕中约定安装船舶相关风险的分配，包括：（1）安装船舶的技术要求；（2）需要使用安装船舶的期间及其为固定或浮动窗口；（3）安装船舶使用时间超出原预计期间的费用分担；及（四）安装船舶未按约定要求运行或不可用时的救济措施。

5.6 For onshore projects, the Contractor shall assume full risk for the supply of the onshore installation cranes required for the installation of the WTGs.

5.6 就陆上项目而言，承包商应承担风电机组安装所需陆上安装起重机供应的全部风险。

5.7 The Employer shall provide payment security to the Contractor in the form of [advance payment security / parent company guarantee / bank guarantee] to the extent agreed in [Appendix ____]. Where the Employer is a special purpose vehicle established solely for the Project, the Employer shall provide such payment security as may be reasonably required by the Contractor having regard to the Employer's creditworthiness.

5.7 雇主应按〔附件____〕约定，以〔预付款担保/母公司担保/银行保函〕形式向承包商提供支付担保。如雇主系仅为项目设立的项目公司（特殊目的载体），雇主应按承包商基于雇主资信合理要求的程度提供相应支付担保。

ARTICLE 6 INTERFACE MANAGEMENT

第六条 接口管理

6.1 Where the Employer engages other contractors in respect of the Wind Farm (including, without limitation, a cables contractor, a foundations contractor or an installation contractor), the Contractor and the Employer shall agree an Interface Schedule regulating the main points of interface between the Contractor and such other contractors.

6.1 如雇主就风电场另行聘用其他承包商（包括但不限于电缆承包商、基础承包商或安装承包商），承包商与雇主应约定一份接口计划，规范承包商与该等其他承包商之间的主要接口事项。

6.2 The Contractor shall comply with the Interface Schedule and shall cooperate with the other contractors in good faith so as to avoid delay and disruption.

6.2 承包商应遵守接口计划，并应善意配合其他承包商，以避免延误与干扰。

6.3 Where the Works are carried out on a disaggregated basis and the Employer retains the interface risk, the Employer shall be responsible for coordinating the various contractors and shall bear the risk of delay and disruption caused by the acts or omissions of such other contractors to the extent that such delay or disruption affects the Contractor's performance under this Agreement.

6.3 如工程以拆解发包方式实施且雇主自留接口风险，雇主应负责协调各承包商，并就因该等其他承包商的作为或不作为对承包商依本合同的履行所造成的延误与干扰承担风险。

6.4 Dispute consolidation: The Parties shall include in the relevant contracts with other contractors provisions for the consolidation of related disputes, with a view to resolving interface-related disputes in a single forum where practicable.

6.4 争议合并：双方应在与其他承包商的相关合同中约定关联争议合并处理的条款，以期在可行时将接口相关争议在单一程序中解决。

ARTICLE 7 PROGRAMME AND DELAYS

第七条 进度计划与延误

7.1 The Contractor shall submit to the Employer, within ____ days after the Effective Date, a detailed programme for the execution of the Works, and shall update the programme at intervals of not more than ____ months.

7.1 承包商应在生效日期后____日内，向雇主提交本工程实施的详细进度计划，并应每隔不超过____个月更新进度计划。

7.2 Time for Completion: The Works shall be taken over by the Employer by ____ (the "Time for Completion"), as adjusted in accordance with this Agreement.

7.2 竣工期限：本工程应于____（下称"竣工期限"）前由雇主接收，并按本合同约定予以调整。

7.3 Delay Liquidated Damages: If the Contractor fails to achieve Take Over of the Wind Farm by the Time for Completion, the Contractor shall pay Delay LDs to the Employer at the rate of ____% of the Contract Price per day (or part thereof), up to a maximum of ____% of the Contract Price in aggregate.

7.3 延误违约金：如承包商未能在竣工期限前实现风电场移交，承包商应按每日合同价格的____%（不足一日按一日计）向雇主支付延误违约金，累计上限为合同价格的____%。

ARTICLE 8 QUALITY ASSURANCE AND WARRANTIES

第八条 质量保证

8.1 The Contractor shall execute the Works in accordance with the specification and standards set out in the Technical Specification, and shall establish a quality assurance and quality control system in accordance with the applicable Laws and international practice.

8.1 承包商应按技术规范所载的规格与标准实施工程，并应依照适用法律及国际惯例建立质量保证与质量控制体系。

8.2 Grid Code Compliance: The Contractor shall, to the extent the requirements of the Grid Code are incorporated in the specification of the WTGs and the Works, be responsible for ensuring that the WTGs and the Works comply with the Grid Code. The Employer shall provide the Contractor with the grid code requirements applicable to the Wind Farm at the time of entering into this Agreement, and the Contractor shall not be liable for grid code compliance to the extent such requirements change after the Base Date unless otherwise agreed.

8.2 电网规范合规：凡电网规范要求已纳入风电机组及工程规格的，承包商应负责确保风电机组及工程符合电网规范。雇主应在签订本合同时向承包商提供适用于风电场的电网规范要求；除另有约定外，基准日之后电网规范要求发生变更的，承包商在该等变更范围内不承担电网规范合规责任。

ARTICLE 9 PROCUREMENT AND SUPPLY

第九条 采购与供应

9.1 The Contractor shall procure all materials, plant and equipment required for the Works, including the WTGs, in accordance with the Technical Specification and the Employer's Requirements, and shall ensure that all procured items are new, of good quality and suitable for their intended purpose.

9.1 承包商应依照技术规范及雇主的要求采购工程所需的全部材料、施工机械与设备（包括风电机组），并应确保所采购物品均为全新、质量良好且适于其预期用途。

9.2 The Contractor shall submit to the Employer for approval the key suppliers and manufacturers proposed for the WTGs and other major equipment. The Employer's approval shall not relieve the Contractor of its obligations under this Agreement.

9.2 承包商应将拟用于风电机组及其他主要设备的关键供应商与制造商提交雇主批准。雇主的批准不免除承包商在本合同项下的义务。

ARTICLE 10 CONSTRUCTION, INSTALLATION AND COMMISSIONING

第十条 施工、安装与调试

10.1 The Contractor shall execute the Works in accordance with the approved programme and the applicable construction methods, and shall comply with all applicable Laws in respect of safety at the Site, including the Regulations on the Administration of Safety of Construction Projects.

10.1 承包商应按经批准的进度计划及适用的施工方法实施工程，并应遵守现场安全方面所有适用的法律，包括《建设工程安全生产管理条例》。

10.2 In accordance with Article 797 of the Civil Code, the Employer shall have the right, at all reasonable times, to inspect the progress and quality of the Works without interfering with the Contractor's normal operations.

10.2 依照《民法典》第七百九十七条，雇主有权在不妨碍承包商正常作业的情况下，随时对本工程的进度与质量进行检查。

第十一条 竣工试验与移交

11.1 Prior to Take Over, each WTG shall: (i) be mechanically complete; (ii) be commissioned; and (iii) achieve test results equivalent to the stipulated minimum level over the relevant number of hours for the Tests on Completion, subject to adjustments for the events set out in the Technical Specification. Unlike traditional power projects, the Tests on Completion under this Agreement shall be of a pass/fail nature rather than requiring the achievement of minimum performance standards for guaranteed values.

11.1 在移交前，每台风电机组应：（1）机械完工；（2）完成调试；及（3）在技术规范规定的相关小时数内取得相当于约定最低水平的试验结果，并按技术规范所载事件予以调整。与传统电力项目不同，本合同项下的竣工试验应为通过/不通过性质，而非要求达到保证值的最低性能标准。

ARTICLE 12 POWER CURVE, NOISE AND AVAILABILITY WARRANTIES

第十二条 功率曲线、噪音与可用率保证

12.1 Power Curve Tests: Power curve tests shall be carried out after Take Over in accordance with the Technical Specification to determine whether the actual power output of the WTGs meets or exceeds the warranted power curve. Where the tests demonstrate that the actual power output over the test period (subject to the applicable adjustments) is less than the warranted power output, the Contractor shall be liable to pay Performance LDs.

12.1 功率曲线试验：应在移交后依照技术规范进行功率曲线试验，以确定风电机组的实际发电功率是否达到或超过保证功率曲线。如试验证明试验期间的实际发电功率（经适用调整后）低于保证功率，承包商应支付性能违约金。

12.2 The Parties shall agree in the Technical Specification the following matters in respect of the power curve tests: (i) the period within which the Employer shall notify the Contractor that it wishes to carry out a power curve test; (ii) the number of retests allowed and the period over which such retests may be carried out; (iii) the events giving rise to adjustments to the warranted power curve; (iv) the number of WTGs to be included in the testing sample so as to be representative of the whole Wind Farm; (v) the duration of the tests; and (vi) the basis for the calculation of the Performance LDs.

12.2 双方应在技术规范中约定与功率曲线试验相关的下列事项：（1）雇主通知承包商其拟进行功率曲线试验的期限；（2）允许复测的次数及复测期间；（3）导致保证功率曲线调整的事件；（4）为使试验样本能代表整个风电场而应纳入测试的风电机组数量；（5）试验时长；及（6）性能违约金的计算基础。
