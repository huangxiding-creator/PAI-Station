Abstract
】
In order to effectively improve the effectiveness and feasibility of the EPC general contracting mode of photovoltaic power station
in controlling project cost, the paper analyzes the factors affecting the project cost control of the EPC general contracting mode of photovoltaic
power station, and explores the cost control measures of the general contracting mode in different stages of project design, bidding, contract
management and project management.
