## ABSTRACT

Based on the historical evolution of China Electric Power Design Institute (CEPDI) and the feasibility of transforming to international engineering companies, this paper uses SWOT to analyze the advantages and disadvantages of CEPDI transformation to international engineering companies, and conducts in-depth research on the feasibility and challenges of CEPDI transformation to international engineering companies. On this basis, the future development direction of CEPDI is clarified: the main direction of transformation and upgrading of China's Electric Power Design Institutes is to transform into international engineering companies.

## Keywords

China Electric Power Design Institute; international engineering companies; SWOT analysis; transformation; Belt and Road
