# 2020版房屋建筑和市政基础设施全过程工程咨询服务合同（中英文对照）

2026-10-12 08:23 四川

2020版房屋建筑和市政基础设施全过程工程咨询服务合同示范文本

2020 EDITION OF THE MODEL TEXT OF THE CONTRACT FOR FULL-PROCESS ENGINEERING CONSULTANCY SERVICES FOR BUILDING AND MUNICIPAL INFRASTRUCTURE

中英文共计4.6万余字，word 文档。

部分章节如下：

根据《中华人民共和国民法典》、《中华人民共和国建筑法》及其他有关法律、法规与规范性文件，遵循自愿、平等、公平和诚信的原则，双方就以下全过程工程咨询服务及有关事项协商一致，订立本合同。

In accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the Construction Law of the People's Republic of China and other applicable laws, regulations and normative documents, and on the principles of voluntariness, equality, fairness and good faith, the Parties hereto have, through consultation, reached agreement on the following whole-process engineering consulting services and related matters and hereby enter into this Contract.

第1条 一般规定

Article 1 General Provisions

1.1 定义和解释

1.1 Definitions and Interpretation

1.1.3 工程合同：是指委托人为实现本项目而与实施永久工程和临时工程的相关承包商、供应商、其他咨询方签订的工程、货物及服务合同。

1.1.3 Engineering Contract: means the contracts for engineering, goods and services entered into by the Client with the relevant contractors, suppliers and other consultants for the implementation of the Permanent Works and Temporary Works for the purpose of realizing the Project.

1.1.4 合同当事人：是指委托人和（或）咨询人。

1.1.4 Parties: means the Client and/or the Consultant.

1.1.5 委托人：是指与咨询人签订合同协议书的，合同中接受咨询服务的一方，及取得该当事人资格的合法继承人和允许的受让人。

1.1.5 Client: means the party which has signed the Contract Agreement with the Consultant and which receives the Consulting Services under the Contract, and includes its lawful successors and permitted assignees.

1.1.6 委托人代表：是指由委托人根据合同约定任命的，在委托人授权范围内代表委托人履行合同的代表。

1.1.6 Client’s Representative: means the representative appointed by the Client in accordance with the Contract and authorised by the Client to act on behalf of the Client within the scope of the authority granted by the Client for the performance of the Contract.

1.1.7 咨询人：是指与委托人签订合同协议书的，合同中提供咨询服务的一方，及取得该当事人资格的合法继承人和允许的受让人。

1.1.7 Consultant: means the party which has signed the Contract Agreement with the Client and which provides the Consulting Services under the Contract, and includes its lawful successors and permitted assignees.

1.1.8 咨询项目总负责人：是指由咨询人根据合同约定任命的，在咨询人授权范围内代表咨询人负责合同履行，主持全过程工程咨询服务工作的负责人。

1.1.8 Consultant’s Project Director: means the person appointed by the Consultant in accordance with the Contract who, within the scope of the authority granted by the Consultant, is responsible for the performance of the Contract on behalf of the Consultant and for directing the Whole-Process Engineering Consulting Services.

1.1.9 单项咨询负责人：是指由咨询人根据合同约定任命的，在咨询人授权范围内代表咨询人负责合同履行，主持相应单项咨询服务工作的负责人。

1.1.9 Consultant’s Representative for an Individual Service: means the person appointed by the Consultant in accordance with the Contract who, within the scope of the authority granted by the Consultant, is responsible for the performance of the Contract on behalf of the Consultant and for directing the relevant individual consulting service.

1.1.10 项目：是指合同协议书中所指的，双方约定由咨询人提供咨询服务的工程项目。

1.1.10 Project: means the engineering project specified in the Contract Agreement in respect of which the Parties have agreed that the Consultant shall provide the Consulting Services.

1.1.11 全过程工程咨询服务：在组成本合同的全部文件中也可简称为咨询服务，是指咨询人接受委托人的委托，并在合同协议书和附件1［服务范围］中明确的，在项目投资决策阶段、工程建设阶段和运营维护阶段，综合运用多学科知识、工程实践经验、现代科学和管理方法，采用多种服务方式组合，为委托人提供阶段性或整体解决方案的综合性智力服务活动。

1.1.11 Whole-Process Engineering Consulting Services: also referred to as the “Consulting Services” in the documents forming the Contract, means the comprehensive intellectual service activities in which the Consultant, as entrusted by the Client and as specified in the Contract Agreement and Annex 1 [Scope of Services], applies multidisciplinary knowledge, engineering practice experience, modern science and management methods in an integrated manner and adopts a combination of various service methods to provide the Client with phased or overall solutions during the project investment decision-making stage, engineering construction stage and operation and maintenance stage.

1.1.12 服务成果：是指附件1［服务范围］所列的，由咨询人为履行咨询服务按照合同约定向委托人提供的有形和无形的服务成果，包括但不限于阶段性和最终的研究、调查、模型、图纸、报告、说明、技术规定和其他类似的电子或实物文件，并应当采用合同中双方约定的载体和形式。

1.1.12 Service Deliverables: means the tangible and intangible deliverables listed in Annex 1 [Scope of Services] to be provided by the Consultant to the Client in accordance with the Contract in the performance of the Consulting Services, including but not limited to interim and final studies, investigations, models, drawings, reports, specifications, technical regulations and other similar electronic or physical documents, which shall be in the medium and form agreed by the Parties in the Contract.

1.1.13 服务变更：是指根据第7条［变更和服务费用调整］构成服务变更的，对于咨询服务的任何更改。

1.1.13 Variation of Services: means any change to the Consulting Services which constitutes a Variation of Services under Article 7 [Variations and Service Fee Adjustment].

1.1.14 服务费用签约价：是指委托人和咨询人在合同协议书中确定的总金额。

1.1.14 Contract Price for Services: means the total amount agreed by the Client and the Consultant in the Contract Agreement.

1.1.15 服务费用：是指委托人用于支付咨询人按照合同约定完成全过程工程咨询服务范围内全部工作的金额，包括合同履行过程中按合同约定进行的变更和调整。

1.1.15 Service Fees: means the amount payable by the Client to the Consultant for the completion of all work within the scope of the Whole-Process Engineering Consulting Services in accordance with the Contract, including variations and adjustments made in accordance with the Contract during the performance of the Contract.

第2条 委托人

Article 2 Client

2.1 委托人一般义务

2.1 General Obligations of the Client

2.1.1 委托人应遵守法律，并办理法律规定由其办理的许可、核准或备案，并将与咨询服务有关的相应结果书面通知咨询人。因委托人原因未能及时办理完毕前述许可、核准或备案手续，导致服务费用增加和（或）服务期限延长时，由委托人承担责任，但因咨询人未能根据合同约定协助委托人办理前述许可、核准或备案手续而导致的除外。

2.1.1 The Client shall comply with the laws and obtain the permits, approvals or record-filings required by law to be obtained by it, and shall notify the Consultant in writing of the corresponding results relating to the Consulting Services. If the Client fails to complete the aforesaid permits, approvals or record-filing procedures in a timely manner for reasons attributable to the Client, resulting in an increase in the Service Fees and/or an extension of the Service Period, the Client shall bear the responsibility, except to the extent caused by the Consultant’s failure to assist the Client in handling the aforesaid permits, approvals or record-filing procedures in accordance with the Contract.

2.2 委托人决定

2.2 Client’s Decisions

除合同另有明确约定外，委托人应根据专用合同条件的约定，在不影响咨询人根据服务进度计划开展咨询服务的时间内，对咨询人以书面形式提出的事项做出书面决定。对咨询人在贯彻落实委托人意见时提出的有关问题，委托人应及时予以解答。因委托人原因未能答复或答复不及时导致服务费用增加和（或）服务期限延长的，由委托人承担。

Unless otherwise expressly stipulated in the Contract, the Client shall, in accordance with the Particular Conditions and within such time as not to affect the Consultant’s performance of the Consulting Services in accordance with the Service Programme, make a written decision on matters raised in writing by the Consultant. The Client shall promptly answer relevant questions raised by the Consultant in implementing the Client’s opinions. If the Service Fees increase and/or the Service Period is extended due to the Client’s failure to reply or delay in reply, the Client shall bear the responsibility.

2.3 支付担保

2.3 Payment Guarantee

除专用合同条件另有约定外，委托人要求咨询人提供履约担保的，委托人应当向咨询人提供支付担保。支付担保可以采用银行保函或保证保险保单或担保公司担保等形式，具体由双方在专用合同条件中约定。

Unless otherwise stipulated in the Particular Conditions, if the Client requires the Consultant to provide Performance Security, the Client shall provide the Consultant with a Payment Guarantee. The Payment Guarantee may take the form of a bank guarantee, a guarantee insurance policy or a guarantee issued by a guarantee company, as specifically agreed by the Parties in the Particular Conditions.

2.4 委托人代表

2.4 Client’s Representative

委托人应指定一位有适当资格和经验的管理人员作为委托人代表，并在专用合同条件中明确其姓名、职务、联系方式及授权范围等事项。委托人代表在委托人的授权范围内，负责处理合同履行过程中与委托人有关的具体事宜。委托人代表在授权范围内的行为由委托人承担法律责任。委托人更换委托人代表的，应在专用合同条件约定的期限内提前书面通知咨询人。

The Client shall appoint a suitably qualified and experienced manager as the Client’s Representative and shall specify in the Particular Conditions its name, position, contact details and scope of authority. The Client’s Representative shall, within the scope of authority granted by the Client, be responsible for handling specific matters relating to the Client during the performance of the Contract. The Client shall bear legal liability for the acts of the Client’s Representative within the scope of its authority. If the Client changes the Client’s Representative, it shall give prior written notice to the Consultant within the period stipulated in the Particular Conditions.

2.5 委托人人员

2.5 Client’s Personnel

委托人应在与咨询人协商后，按照专用合同条件的要求，自费从其雇员中为推进项目安排、选择及提供有适当资格和经验的人员。咨询人可对上述人员的选择和替换提出合理异议，且在与相关法律法规不发生冲突的前提下，上述人员应就与咨询服务有关的事项接受咨询人的指示。

The Client shall, after consultation with the Consultant and at its own expense, arrange, select and provide from among its employees suitably qualified and experienced personnel for the Project in accordance with the requirements of the Particular Conditions. The Consultant may raise reasonable objections to the choice and replacement of such personnel, and, provided that no conflict with applicable laws and regulations arises, such personnel shall follow the instructions of the Consultant on matters relating to the Consulting Services.

第3条 咨询人

Article 3 Consultant

3.1 咨询人一般义务

3.1 General Obligations of the Consultant

3.1.1 咨询人应根据合同约定以及附件1［服务范围］约定的咨询服务内容和要求提供咨询服务。如附件1［服务范围］中未详细描述咨询人的工作，则咨询人应为满足附件1［服务范围］中描述的所有功能和目的而履行咨询服务。

3.1.1 The Consultant shall provide the Consulting Services in accordance with the Contract and the content and requirements of the Consulting Services set out in Annex 1 [Scope of Services]. If the Consultant’s work is not described in detail in Annex 1 [Scope of Services], the Consultant shall perform the Consulting Services so as to satisfy all functions and purposes described in Annex 1 [Scope of Services].

3.1.2 咨询人应按照附件4［咨询人主要咨询人员］组建能够满足咨询服务需要的咨询服务机构，并按照附件3［进度计划］的约定完成咨询服务。

3.1.2 The Consultant shall establish a consulting services organization capable of meeting the requirements of the Consulting Services in accordance with Annex 4 [Key Personnel of the Consultant], and shall complete the Consulting Services in accordance with Annex 3 [Programme].

3.1.3 委托人需要咨询人提供履约担保的，应当按照相关规定在专用合同条件中明确约定，咨询人应按照约定的形式、金额及期限等向委托人提供履约担保。

3.1.3 Where the Client requires the Consultant to provide Performance Security, it shall be expressly stipulated in the Particular Conditions in accordance with the relevant provisions, and the Consultant shall provide the Performance Security to the Client in the agreed form, amount and time limit.

4.3 服务成果的交付

4.3 Delivery of Service Deliverables

4.3.1 咨询人应按照附件3［进度计划］约定的服务成果交付时间向委托人交付服务成果，委托人应当出具书面签收单。

4.3.1 The Consultant shall deliver the Service Deliverables to the Client at the time for delivery stipulated in Annex 3 [Programme], and the Client shall issue a written receipt.

4.3.2 委托人要求咨询人提前交付服务成果的，应向咨询人下达提前交付的书面通知并明确提前交付的内容，但委托人不得压缩合理的服务期限。咨询人应向委托人提交提前交付服务成果建议书，该建议书应包括实施的方案、缩短的时间、增加的服务费用等内容。委托人接受该建议书的，咨询人应按照该建议书修订咨询服务进度计划，由此增加的服务费用由委托人承担。咨询人认为提前交付服务成果无法执行的，应向委托人提出书面异议，委托人应在收到异议后7天内予以答复，7天内未予答复的，视为委托人认可咨询人的书面异议。

4.3.2 If the Client requires the Consultant to deliver the Service Deliverables in advance, it shall issue a written notice of early delivery to the Consultant and specify the content of the early delivery; provided that the Client shall not compress the reasonable Service Period. The Consultant shall submit to the Client a proposal for early delivery of the Service Deliverables, which shall include the implementation plan, reduction in time, additional Service Fees and other relevant matters. If the Client accepts the proposal, the Consultant shall revise the Service Programme in accordance with the proposal, and the additional Service Fees shall be borne by the Client. If the Consultant considers early delivery of the Service Deliverables impracticable, it shall submit a written objection to the Client. The Client shall reply within seven days after receipt of the objection. If the Client fails to reply within seven days, it shall be deemed to have approved the Consultant’s written objection.

4.3.3 委托人要求咨询人提前交付服务成果的，或咨询人提出提前交付服务成果的建议能够给委托人带来效益的，合同当事人可以在附件2［服务费用和支付］中约定对提前交付服务成果的奖励。

4.3.3 If the Client requires the Consultant to deliver the Service Deliverables in advance, or if the Consultant’s proposal for early delivery of the Service Deliverables can bring benefits to the Client, the Parties may agree in Annex 2 [Service Fees and Payment] on a reward for early delivery of the Service Deliverables.

第5条 进度计划、延误和暂停

Article 5 Programme, Delays and Suspension

5.1 服务的开始和完成

5.1 Commencement and Completion of the Services

5.1.1 委托人应在计划开始服务日期7天前向咨询人发出开始服务工作通知，服务期限自开始服务通知中载明的日期或按照专用合同条件约定的日期起算。咨询服务应在专用合同条件所约定的时间或期限内开始和完成，根据合同约定进行延期的除外。

5.1.1 The Client shall issue a notice to commence the Services to the Consultant seven days prior to the scheduled service commencement date, and the Service Period shall commence from the date specified in the notice to commence the Services or the date stipulated in the Particular Conditions. The Consulting Services shall commence and be completed within the time or time limit stipulated in the Particular Conditions, except for extensions in accordance with the Contract.

5.2 服务进度计划

5.2 Service Programme

5.2.1 咨询人应按照双方在专用合同条件中的约定，提交服务进度计划，计划应至少包括：

5.2.1 The Consultant shall submit a Service Programme in accordance with the agreement between the Parties in the Particular Conditions. The Programme shall include at least:

（1）咨询人为按时完成所有咨询服务而计划开展各项咨询服务的顺序和时点；

(1) the sequence and timing of the various Consulting Services scheduled by the Consultant to complete all Consulting Services on time;

（2）附件3［进度计划］或合同其他部分所约定的将各项服务成果交付委托人的关键日期；

(2) the key dates for delivery of the Service Deliverables to the Client as stipulated in Annex 3 [Programme] or other parts of the Contract;

第6条 服务费用和支付

Article 6 Service Fees and Payment

6.1 服务费用

6.1 Service Fees

6.1.1 委托人和咨询人应当在附件2［服务费用和支付］中明确约定服务费用的组成部分和计取方式，包括变更和调整的计取方式。

6.1.1 The Client and the Consultant shall expressly specify in Annex 2 [Service Fees and Payment] the components of the Service Fees and the method of calculation, including the method of calculation of variations and adjustments.

6.1.2 除附件2［服务费用和支付］另有约定外，合同下约定的服务费用均已包含国家规定的增值税税金。

6.1.2 Unless otherwise stipulated in Annex 2 [Service Fees and Payment], the Service Fees stipulated in the Contract include the value-added tax prescribed by the State.

6.1.3 委托人和咨询人应当在附件2［服务费用和支付］中明确约定咨询人为履行合同发生的差旅费、通讯费、复印费、材料和设备检测费等服务开支是否已包含在服务酬金内，以及服务酬金中未包括的服务开支的计取和支付方法。

6.1.3 The Client and the Consultant shall expressly stipulate in Annex 2 [Service Fees and Payment] whether the Service Expenses incurred by the Consultant in the performance of the Contract, such as travel expenses, communication expenses, copying expenses and materials and equipment testing expenses, are included in the Service Fees, and the method of calculation and payment of Service Expenses not included in the Service Fees.

6.1.4 对于咨询人在服务过程中提出合理化建议并被委托人采纳，以及咨询人提供咨询服务节约本项目投资额、咨询人提前交付服务成果等使委托人获得效益或规避潜在风险的情形，双方可在附件2［服务费用和支付］中约定奖励金额的计取和支付方法。

6.1.4 The Parties may agree in Annex 2 [Service Fees and Payment] on the method of calculation and payment of rewards in circumstances where the Consultant’s reasonable suggestions made in the course of the Services are adopted by the Client, or where the Consultant’s provision of the Consulting Services saves investment in the Project or the Consultant delivers the Service Deliverables in advance, thereby enabling the Client to obtain benefits or avoid potential risks.

6.2 支付程序和方式

6.2 Payment Procedures and Methods

6.2.1 咨询人应在附件2［服务费用和支付］约定的每个应付款日的至少7天前，向委托人提交支付申请书。支付申请书应包括下列款项的金额及明细：

6.2.1 The Consultant shall submit a payment application to the Client at least seven days before each payment due date stipulated in Annex 2 [Service Fees and Payment]. The payment application shall include the amounts and details of the following:

（1）当期已经完成的咨询服务对应的服务酬金；

(1) the Service Fees corresponding to the Consulting Services completed in the current period;

（2）根据附件2［服务费用和支付］约定，咨询人为提供咨询服务所产生的、不包含在服务酬金内的合理服务开支；

(2) the reasonable Service Expenses incurred by the Consultant in providing the Consulting Services that are not included in the Service Fees, as stipulated in Annex 2 [Service Fees and Payment];

（3）根据附件2［服务费用和支付］的约定，根据咨询人提供咨询服务节约的投资额等标准对咨询人进行的奖励金额；

(3) the amount of any reward payable to the Consultant in accordance with Annex 2 [Service Fees and Payment] based on criteria such as the amount of investment saved through the Consultant’s provision of the Consulting Services;
