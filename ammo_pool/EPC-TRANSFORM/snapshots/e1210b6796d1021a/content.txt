英文释义：Spain's Técnicas Reunidas secured >$5.75bn EPCI packages from ADNOC for Upper Zakum (to expand capacity to 1.5 mn b/d) plus a $1.65bn lump-sum turnkey onshore gas-processing facility — its largest sole-contractor awards to date.
