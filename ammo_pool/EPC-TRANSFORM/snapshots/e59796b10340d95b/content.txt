# 上海宝冶国际工程公司2026年度下半年项目预招标公告

2026-07-10 17:16 上海

上海宝冶国际工程公司

2026年度下半年项目预招标公告

上海宝冶国际工程公司计划于2026年度下半年开展一系列分包、物资及服务的招标采购工作。现将部分项目的初步招标计划预告如下，诚邀各领域优秀的供应商、承包商（以下简称“应标方”）关注并参与。

一、招标内容简介

二、招标相关说明

一、 意向应标方基本要求

1.合法资质：在境内或境外项目所在地合法注册并持续经营，具备所投业务领域的相应资质与许可。

2.经验业绩：拥有类似项目或同类型业务的成功实施经验与良好业绩记录。

3.财务能力：财务状况良好，具备履行合同所需的资金保障能力。

4.本地资源：熟悉项目所在地当地市场、法律法规及施工环境，拥有稳定的本地人力资源和供应链支持者将获优先考虑。

5.合规诚信：信誉良好，无重大诉讼及不良履约记录，承诺遵守商业道德与反腐败法规。

二、 预报名与接洽

有意参与上海宝冶国际工程公司项目合作的应标方，请按以下步骤进行预报名：

提交预审资料：请将贵公司的中英文简介、营业执照、资质证书、近三年主要业绩证明、联系人及联系方式等信息，整理为一份PDF文件。

发送邮箱：将上述资料发送至我司指定预审邮箱：gjzbb@sbc-mcc.com。

邮件主题格式：请统一命名为“【2026预报名】-公司全称-主营类别”（例如：【2026预报名】-ABC建筑有限公司-土建分包）。

配合入库：本次报名及后续参与任何项目的询价、招标，即视为同意配合我司审核，并同意将贵司信息录入“上海宝冶国际工程公司合格分包商/供应商资源库”，作为未来项目合作的重要参考。

接洽沟通：我司招采部门在收到资料并进行初步筛选后，将视情况与符合条件的应标方联系，安排进一步沟通或组织项目踏勘、澄清会等。

三、 重要声明

1.本公告为预招标通知，不构成任何招标或要约。具体项目的正式招标信息，以上海宝冶集团国际工程公司后续通过正式渠道（包括但不限于本公司官网、微信公众号、指定邮箱）发布的《招标公告》及《招标文件》为准。

2.上海宝冶国际工程公司保留对此次招标计划进行调整、修改或取消部分招标项目的权利，且无需对此承担任何责任。

3.应标方提交的所有预审资料将被保密处理，并仅用于本次预招标资格评估及资源库入库评审，评估结果恕不另行通知。

Pre-Tender Announcement for Projects in the Second Half of 2026Issued by Shanghai Baoye International Engineering Corporation

ShanghaiBaoye InternationalEngineering Corporation plans to launch a series of bidding and procurement activities for subcontracting, materials and services in the second half of 2026. We hereby release the preliminary tender plan for partial projects as follows, sincerely invitingoutstandingsuppliers and contractors (hereinafter referred to as Bidders) from all sectors to pay attention and participate.

I. Basic Requirements for Interested Bidders

1.Legal Qualification: Legally registered and continuously operated within China or the host country of the project, with valid qualifications and permits corresponding to the tendered business scope.

2.Experience & Performance: Having successful implementation experience and a good performance record in similar projects or business types.

3.Financial Capability: Sound financial status with sufficient financial resources to fulfill the contract.

4.Local Resources: Priority will be given to bidders familiar with local market, laws & regulations and construction environment of the project location, with stable local manpower and supply chain support.

5.Compliance & Integrity: Good reputation, no major litigation or record of poor performance, committed to complying with business ethics and anti-corruption regulations.

II. Pre-Registration and Engagement

Bidders intending to cooperate with Shanghai Baoye International Engineering Corporation shall complete pre-registration following the procedures below:

1.Submit Pre-qualification Documents: Compile a single PDF file containing Chinese & English company profile, business license, qualification certificates, key project performance records in the past three years, contact person and contact information.

2.Send Email: Send the above documents to the designated prequalification mailbox: gjzbb@sbc-mcc.com.

3.Email Subject Format: Please use the uniform format: "【2026 Pre-registration】- Full Company Name - Main Business Category" (e.g., 【2026 Pre-registration】- ABC Construction Co., Ltd. - Civil Subcontracting).

4.Agreement for Supplier Registration: By submitting this registration and participating in subsequent inquiries or tenders, the bidder consents to our company’s qualification review and inclusion into the Qualified Subcontractor & Supplier Resource Database of Shanghai Baoye International Engineering Corporation, which will serve as a key reference for future project cooperation.

5.Engagement & Communication: Our procurement department will review the received documents. Qualified bidders may be contacted for further communication, site visits, or clarification meetings as necessary.

III. Important Notes

1.This is a Pre-Bidding Notice and does not constitute any bidding invitation or offer. Official bidding information for specific projects shall be subject to the subsequent "Tender Notice"and "Tender Documents"issued by Shanghai Baoye International Engineering Corporation through formal channels (including but not limited to the company's official website, WeChat official account, and designated email).

2.Shanghai Baoye International Engineering Corporation reserves the right to adjust, modify, or cancel any part of the bidding plan without assuming any liability thereof.

3.All pre-qualification documents submitted by bidders will be treated confidentially and used solely for the evaluation in this pre-bidding process and database registration. The evaluation results will not be notified separately.
