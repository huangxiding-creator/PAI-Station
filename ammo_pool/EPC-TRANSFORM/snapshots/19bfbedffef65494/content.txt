根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国建筑法》《中华人民共和国招标投标法》及相关法律法规，遵循平等、自愿、公平和诚信的原则，双方就_______项目工程建设全过程咨询服务及有关事项协商一致，共同达成如下协议：

In accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, the Construction Law of the People's Republic of China, the Tendering and Bidding Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant laws and regulations, and in accordance with the principles of equality, voluntariness, fairness and good faith, the Parties hereto have reached the following agreement in respect of the whole-process engineering consultancy services for the _______ Project and related matters:

第2条 委托人

Article 2 Client

1.1委托人一般义务

2.1 General Obligations of the Client

2.1.1委托人应遵守法律，并办理法律规定由其办理的许可、核准或备案，并将与咨询服务有关的相应结果书面通知咨询人。因委托人原因未能及时办理完毕前述许可、核准或备案手续，导致服务费用增加和（或）服务期限延长时，由委托人承担责任，但因咨询人未能根据合同约定协助委托人办理前述许可、核准或备案手续而导致的除外。

2.1.1 The Client shall comply with laws and handle the permits, approvals or filings required by law to be handled by it, and shall notify the Consultant in writing of the corresponding results relating to the Consultancy Services. If the Client fails to complete the aforementioned permit, approval or filing procedures in a timely manner, resulting in an increase in Service Fees and/or an extension of the Service Period, the Client shall bear liability therefor, unless such failure is caused by the Consultant's failure to assist the Client in handling the aforementioned permit, approval or filing procedures in accordance with the Contract.

2.1.2除合同另有约定外，委托人应向受托人提供咨询服务所涉及的必要外部关系的协调以及与其他组织联系的信息和方式，以便受托人收集需要的信息，为受托人履行职责提供外部条件。委托人应在工程合同中或根据工程合同约定及时向相关承包商、供应商、其他咨询方等提供受托人及咨询项目总负责人的名称或姓名、服务内容、权限及其他必要信息，并负责就受托人与委托人以及委托人的相关承包商、供应商、其他咨询方等之间的职权边界予以协调和明确。

2.1.2 Except as otherwise stipulated in the Contract, the Client shall provide the Consultant with coordination of necessary external relations involved in the Consultancy Services and information on and means of contact with other organizations, so that the Consultant may collect the required information and shall provide external conditions for the Consultant to perform its duties. The Client shall, under the engineering contracts or in accordance with the provisions thereof, timely provide the relevant contractors, suppliers, other consultants and other parties with the name, service content, authority and other necessary information of the Consultant and the Chief Consulting Project Manager, and shall be responsible for coordinating and clarifying the boundaries of authority between the Consultant and the Client and the Client's relevant contractors, suppliers, other consultants and other parties.

第1条 受托人

Article 3 Consultant

3.1 受托人一般义务

3.1 General Obligations of the Consultant

3.1.1受托人应根据本合同约定的咨询服务内容和要求提供咨询服务。

3.1.1 The Consultant shall provide the Consultancy Services in accordance with the content and requirements of the Consultancy Services stipulated in the Contract.

3.1.2受托人应按照本合同约定组建能够满足咨询服务需要的咨询服务机构并完成咨询服务。

3.1.2 The Consultant shall, in accordance with the Contract, establish a consulting services organization capable of meeting the needs of the Consultancy Services and complete the Consultancy Services.

3.1.3受托人在履行合同义务时，应严格按照法律法规、强制性标准及合同约定，谨慎、勤勉地履行职责，维护委托人的合法利益，保证服务成果质量。

3.1.3 The Consultant shall, in performing its contractual obligations, perform its duties prudently and diligently in strict accordance with laws, regulations, mandatory standards and the Contract, safeguard the lawful interests of the Client, and ensure the quality of the Deliverables.

3.1.4受托人及其咨询人员应满足法律法规有关规定。在保证整个工程项目完整性的前提下，由受托人按照第3.4款［委托其他咨询单位实施咨询服务］的约定将自有资质证书许可范围外的咨询业务依法依规择优委托其他咨询单位实施的，该被委托的其他咨询单位应具有相应能力或水平。

3.1.4 The Consultant and its consulting personnel shall comply with the relevant provisions of laws and regulations. On the premise of ensuring the integrity of the entire Project, if the Consultant entrusts consulting business outside the scope permitted by its own qualification certificate to other consulting entities on the basis of merit in accordance with laws and regulations and in accordance with Article 3.4 [Entrusting Other Consulting Entities to Perform Consultancy Services], such other consulting entities shall have corresponding competence or capability.

3.1.5在履行合同期间，受托人应使委托人保持对咨询服务进展的了解，并定期向委托人报告咨询服务工作进展。

3.1.5 During the performance of the Contract, the Consultant shall keep the Client informed of the progress of the Consultancy Services and shall regularly report to the Client on the progress of the Consultancy Services.

3.1.6任何由委托人支付费用并提供给受托人使用的物品均属于委托人财产。受托人有权无偿使用第2.1.3项中由委托人提供的设备、设施、资料。受托人应采取合理的措施保护委托人提供的设备、设施及其他财产，直至咨询服务完成并将其退还给委托人。保护委托人财产所产生的合理费用应由委托人承担。

3.1.6 Any items paid for by the Client and provided to the Consultant for use shall be the property of the Client. The Consultant shall have the right to use, without charge, the equipment, facilities and materials provided by the Client under Article 2.1.3. The Consultant shall take reasonable measures to protect the Client's property until the Consultancy Services are completed and shall return the same to the Client. Reasonable costs incurred in protecting the Client's property shall be borne by the Client.

3.1.7合同当事人可在专用合同条款约定受托人应承担的其他义务。

3.1.7 The Parties may agree in the Particular Conditions on other obligations to be undertaken by the Consultant.

3.2 咨询项目总负责人

3.2 Chief Consulting Project Manager

3.2.1咨询项目总负责人应为合同协议书及专用合同条款中约定的人选，并应具有履行相应职责的资格、能力和经验。双方应在合同协议书及专用合同条款中明确咨询项目总负责人的基本信息及授权范围等事项。

3.2.1 The Chief Consulting Project Manager shall be the person agreed in the Contract Agreement and the Particular Conditions and shall have the qualifications, competence and experience to perform the corresponding duties. The Parties shall specify in the Contract Agreement and the Particular Conditions the basic information and scope of authority of the Chief Consulting Project Manager.

3.2.2受托人需要更换咨询项目总负责人的，应在专用合同条款约定的期限内提前书面通知委托人，并征得委托人书面同意。受托人擅自更换咨询项目总负责人的，应按照专用合同条款的约定承担违约责任。

3.2.2 If the Consultant needs to replace the Chief Consulting Project Manager, it shall notify the Client in writing in advance within the period agreed in the Particular Conditions and obtain the Client's written consent. If the Consultant replaces the Chief Consulting Project Manager without authorization, it shall bear liability for breach of contract in accordance with the Particular Conditions.

3.2.3委托人有权书面通知受托人更换不称职的咨询项目总负责人，通知中应载明要求更换的理由。受托人无正当理由拒绝更换咨询项目总负责人的，应按照专用合同条款约定承担违约责任。

3.2.3 The Client shall have the right to notify the Consultant in writing to replace an incompetent Chief Consulting Project Manager, and the notice shall state the reasons for the replacement. If the Consultant refuses to replace the Chief Consulting Project Manager without justified reason, it shall bear liability for breach of contract in accordance with the Particular Conditions.

3.3 咨询人员

3.3 Consulting Personnel

3.3.1 咨询人员包括咨询项目总负责人及其他由受托人配备和派遣，提供咨询服务的人员。受托人应按照合同约定，根据咨询服务需求配备和派遣具备相应能力和经验的各专项咨询负责人，以及其他专业技术人员和管理人员。双方应在专用合同条款中明确各专项咨询负责人的基本信息及授权范围等事项。

3.3.1 The Consulting Personnel include the Chief Consulting Project Manager and other personnel provided and dispatched by the Consultant to perform the Consultancy Services. The Consultant shall, in accordance with the Contract and the requirements of the Consultancy Services, provide and dispatch Heads of Specialized Consultancy with appropriate competence and experience, as well as other professional and technical personnel and management personnel. The Parties shall specify in the Particular Conditions the basic information and scope of authority of each Head of Specialized Consultancy.

第4条 咨询服务要求及成果

Article 4 Requirements for and Deliverables of Consultancy Services

4.1 咨询服务依据

4.1 Basis for Consultancy Services

4.1.1 委托人应根据合同约定，向受托人提供与咨询服务有关的资料和信息。委托人提供上述资料和信息超过约定期限，导致服务开支增加和（或）服务期限延长的，由委托人承担责任。

4.1.1 The Client shall, in accordance with the Contract, provide the Consultant with data and information relating to the Consultancy Services. If the Client provides such data and information beyond the agreed time limit, resulting in an increase in Service Expenses and/or an extension of the Service Period, the Client shall bear liability therefor.

4.1.2 委托人应对所提供资料和信息的真实性、准确性、合法性与完整性负责。委托人未按照合同约定提供必要的资料和信息，影响服务成果质量或导致服务开支增加和（或）服务期限延长的，由委托人承担责任。

4.1.2 The Client shall be responsible for the authenticity, accuracy, legality and completeness of the data and information provided. If the Client fails to provide the necessary data and information in accordance with the Contract, thereby affecting the quality of the Deliverables or causing an increase in Service Expenses and/or an extension of the Service Period, the Client shall bear liability therefor.

4.1.3 任何一方发现委托人所提供的资料和信息存在错误、疏漏或问题的，应及时通知另一方，但对上述错误、疏漏或问题的纠正应经委托人确认。

4.1.3 If either Party discovers any error, omission or problem in the data and information provided by the Client, it shall promptly notify the other Party; however, the correction of such error, omission or problem shall be subject to confirmation by the Client.

4.1.4 委托人应遵守法律法规和技术标准，不得以任何理由要求受托人违反法律法规，压缩合理服务期限，降低技术标准和工程质量、安全标准提供咨询服务。咨询服务有关的特殊标准和要求由双方在专用合同条款中约定。

4.1.4 The Client shall comply with laws, regulations and technical standards, and shall not for any reason require the Consultant to violate laws or regulations, shorten the reasonable Service Period, or lower technical standards, engineering quality or safety standards in providing the Consultancy Services. Special standards and requirements relating to the Consultancy Services shall be agreed by the Parties in the Particular Conditions.

4.1.5 委托人对主要技术指标有要求的，经委托人与受托人协商一致后应在专用合同条款中约定。因委托人原因导致主要技术指标变更的，委托人应承担相应责任。因受托人原因导致主要技术指标未达到合同要求的，受托人应承担相应违约责任。

4.1.5 If the Client has requirements for key technical specifications, such requirements shall be agreed in the Particular Conditions after consensus is reached between the Client and the Consultant. If key technical specifications are changed due to the Client's reasons, the Client shall bear corresponding liability. If key technical specifications fail to meet the requirements of the Contract due to the Consultant's reasons, the Consultant shall bear corresponding liability for breach of Contract.

4.2 咨询服务成果要求

4.2 Requirements for Deliverables

4.2.1 服务成果应符合法律法规、相关标准及合同约定。咨询服务成果具体内容和要求在专用合同条款中约定。

4.2.1 The Deliverables shall comply with laws, regulations, relevant standards and the Contract. The specific contents and requirements of the Deliverables shall be agreed in the Particular Conditions.

4.2.2 受托人应对其咨询服务成果的真实性、有效性和科学性负责。因受托人原因造成咨询服务成果不合格的，委托人有权要求受托人采取补救措施，直至达到合同要求的质量标准，受托人应按合同约定承担相应违约责任。

4.2.2 The Consultant shall be responsible for the authenticity, validity and scientific soundness of its Deliverables. If the Deliverables are unqualified due to the Consultant's reasons, the Client shall have the right to require the Consultant to take remedial measures until the quality standards required by the Contract are met, and the Consultant shall bear corresponding liability for breach of Contract in accordance with the Contract.

第5条 进度计划、延误和暂停

Article 5 Schedule, Delays and Suspension

5.1 服务的开始和完成

5.1 Commencement and Completion of Services

5.1.1 委托人应在计划开始服务日期7天前向咨询人发出开始服务工作通知，服务期限自开始服务通知中载明的日期或按照专用合同条件约定的日期起算。

5.1.1 The Client shall issue a notice to commence the Services to the Consultant not less than seven days before the scheduled commencement date of the Services, and the Service Period shall commence from the date specified in the notice to commence the Services or the date agreed in accordance with the Particular Conditions.

5.1.2 咨询服务应在专用合同条款约定的时间或期限内开始和完成，按照合同约定延期的除外。

5.1.2 The Consultancy Services shall commence and be completed within the time or period agreed in the Particular Conditions, except for any extension in accordance with the Contract.

5.2 服务进度计划

5.2 Service Schedule

5.2.1 受托人应按照专用合同条款约定，提交服务进度计划。服务进度计划应至少包括下列内容：

5.2.1 The Consultant shall submit a Service Schedule in accordance with the Particular Conditions. The Service Schedule shall include at least the following:

（1）受托人为按时完成所有咨询服务而计划开展的各项咨询服务顺序和时间；

(1) the sequence and timing of each Consultancy Service that the Consultant plans to perform in order to complete all Consultancy Services on schedule;

（2）合同约定的各项咨询服务成果交付委托人的关键日期；

(2) the key dates for delivery to the Client of the Deliverables for each Consultancy Service agreed in the Contract;

（3）需要委托人或第三方提供决策、同意、批准或资料的关键日期；

(3) the key dates by which decisions, consents, approvals or information are required from the Client or third parties;

（4）合同关于进度计划条款约定的其他要求。

(4) other requirements stipulated in the provisions of the Contract relating to the schedule.

5.2.2 除专用合同条款另有约定外，委托人应在收到受托人提交的服务进度计划后14天内完成审批或提出异议。逾期未审批或提出异议的，视为委托人认可该服务进度计划。

5.2.2 Unless otherwise stipulated in the Particular Conditions, the Client shall complete its approval or raise an objection within 14 days after receipt of the Service Schedule submitted by the Consultant. If the Client fails to approve or raise an objection within such period, the Client shall be deemed to have approved the Service Schedule.

5.2.3 项目实际进度与服务进度计划不一致的，受托人应及时向委托人提交修订的服务进度计划，并附相关措施和资料。除专用合同条款另有约定外，委托人应在收到修订的服务进度计划后7天内完成审批或提出异议。逾期未审批或提出异议的，视为委托人认可修订后的服务进度计划。

5.2.3 If the actual progress of the Project is inconsistent with the Service Schedule, the Consultant shall promptly submit a revised Service Schedule to the Client, together with relevant measures and information. Unless otherwise stipulated in the Particular Conditions, the Client shall complete its approval or raise an objection within seven days after receipt of the revised Service Schedule. If the Client fails to approve or raise an objection within such period, the Client shall be deemed to have approved the revised Service Schedule.

5.2.4 对于任何可能对服务产生不利影响、导致费用增加或服务进度计划延误的事件或情况，任何一方在得知上述事件或情况后应立即向另一方发出通知。

5.2.4 If either Party becomes aware of any event or circumstance that may adversely affect the Services, cause an increase in Service Fees or delay the Service Schedule, it shall immediately notify the other Party.

5.3 服务进度延误

5.3 Delay in Service Progress

5.3.1 合同履行过程中，因发生下列情形造成咨询服务进度延误，属于非受托人原因导致的咨询服务进度延误：

5.3.1 During the performance of the Contract, delay in the progress of the Consultancy Services caused by any of the following circumstances shall constitute delay not attributable to the Consultant:

（1）委托人未能按合同约定提供有关资料或所提供的有关资料不符合合同约定或存在错误或疏漏的；

(1) the Client fails to provide relevant information in accordance with the Contract, or the relevant information provided does not comply with the Contract or contains errors or omissions;

（2）委托人未能按合同约定提供咨询服务工作条件、设施场地、人员服务的；

(2) the Client fails to provide the working conditions, facilities and site, and personnel services for the Consultancy Services in accordance with the Contract;

（3）委托人对咨询服务的服务变更；

(3) a Variation to the Consultancy Services instructed by the Client;

（4）委托人或委托人的承包商、供应商、其他咨询方等使咨询服务受到障碍或延误的；

(4) the Client or its contractors, suppliers, other consultants or other parties hinders or delays the Consultancy Services;

（5）委托人未按合同约定日期足额付款的；

(5) the Client fails to pay in full on the date agreed in the Contract;

第6条 服务费用和支付

Article 6 Service Fees and Payment

6.1 服务费用

6.1 Service Fees

6.1.1 委托人和受托人应在合同中明确约定服务费用的组成和计取方式，包括变更和调整的计取方式。

6.1.1 The Client and the Consultant shall clearly agree in the Contract on the composition and calculation of the Service Fees, including the calculation of Variations and adjustments.

6.1.2 除合同另有约定外，合同约定的服务费用均已包含国家规定的增值税税金。

6.1.2 Unless otherwise stipulated in the Contract, the Service Fees agreed in the Contract are inclusive of value-added tax prescribed by the State.

6.1.3 委托人和受托人应在合同中明确约定受托人为履行合同发生的差旅费、通讯费、复印费、材料和设备检测费等服务开支是否已包含在服务费用内，以及服务费用中未包括的服务开支的计取和支付方式。

6.1.3 The Client and the Consultant shall clearly agree in the Contract whether Service Expenses incurred by the Consultant for the performance of the Contract, such as travel expenses, communication expenses, copying expenses and materials and equipment testing expenses, are included in the Service Fees, and the method of calculation and payment of Service Expenses not included in the Service Fees.

6.1.4 对于受托人在咨询服务过程中提出合理化建议并被委托人采纳，以及受托人提供咨询服务节约项目投资额、受托人提前交付服务成果等使委托人获得效益或规避潜在风险的情形，双方可在合同中约定奖励机制和奖励费用的计取、支付方式。

6.1.4 Where the Consultant makes reasonable proposals during the Consultancy Services and such proposals are adopted by the Client, or where the Consultant's provision of Consultancy Services saves project investment or the Consultant delivers the Deliverables ahead of schedule, thereby bringing benefits to the Client or avoiding potential risks, the Parties may agree in the Contract on an incentive mechanism and the method of calculation and payment of incentive amounts.

6.2 支付程序和方式

6.2 Payment Procedures and Methods

6.2.1 受托人应在合同约定的每一个应付款日的至少7天前，向委托人提交支付申请书，并附必要的证明材料复印件。支付申请书应包括下列款项的金额及明细：

6.2.1 The Consultant shall, at least seven days before each payment due date agreed in the Contract, submit to the Client an application for payment together with copies of necessary supporting documents. The application for payment shall include the amounts and breakdown of the following:

（1）当期已完成咨询服务对应的服务费用；

(1) Service Fees corresponding to the Consultancy Services completed in the current period;

（2）受托人为提供咨询服务所产生的、不包含在服务费用内的合理服务开支；

(2) reasonable Service Expenses incurred by the Consultant in providing the Consultancy Services that are not included in the Service Fees;

（3）受托人提供咨询服务节约项目投资额等，根据合同约定应对受托人进行奖励的金额；

(3) incentive amounts payable to the Consultant in accordance with the Contract, such as for saving project investment through the provision of Consultancy Services;

（4）应增加或扣减的变更调整金额；

(4) amounts of Variations and adjustments to be added or deducted;

（5）根据第11条［违约责任］约定应增加或扣减的索赔或违约金额；

(5) amounts of claims or liquidated damages to be added or deducted in accordance with Article 11 [Liability for Breach of Contract];

（6）对已付款中出现的错误、遗漏或重复的修订，应在当期付款中支付或扣除的金额；

(6) amounts to be paid or deducted in the current payment for corrections of errors, omissions or duplications in previous payments;

（7）合同约定应增加和扣减的其他金额。

(7) other amounts to be added or deducted as agreed in the Contract.

6.2.2 支付申请书中的服务费用、服务开支和奖励金额等款项应分列，报送委托人审核并支付。对于合同约定的服务费用以外发生的服务开支，应随服务开支发生的该月或该阶段的服务费用一并在支付申请书中提交，并经委托人审核后支付。

6.2.2 The Service Fees, Service Expenses and incentive amounts in the application for payment shall be itemized separately and submitted to the Client for review and payment. Service Expenses incurred other than the Service Fees agreed in the Contract shall be submitted in the application for payment together with the Service Fees for the month or phase in which such Service Expenses are incurred, and shall be paid after review by the Client.

6.2.3 在对已付款进行汇总和复核过程中发现错误、遗漏或重复的，委托人和受托人均有权提出修正申请。经对方同意的修正，应在下期付款中支付或扣除。

6.2.3 If errors, omissions or duplications are found in the course of summarizing and reviewing payments already made, either the Client or the Consultant shall have the right to submit a request for correction. 6.3 有争议部分的付款

6.3 Payment of Disputed Amounts

委托人对受托人提交的支付申请书有异议时，应在收到受托人提交的支付申请书后7天内，以书面形式向受托人发出异议通知，并说明有异议部分款项的数额及理由。无异议部分的款项应按期支付，有异议部分的款项按第13条［争议解决］约定办理。对双方最终确定应支付给受托人的有异议款项，仍应适用第6.2款［支付程序和方式］的约定。

If the Client has any objection to an application for payment submitted by the Consultant, it shall, within seven days after receipt of such application, give a written notice of objection to the Consultant, stating the amount and reasons for the objection. The undisputed amount shall be paid on time, and the disputed amount shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 13 [Dispute Resolution]. Any disputed amount finally determined by the Parties as payable to the Consultant shall remain subject to Article 6.2 [Payment Procedures and Methods].

6.4.2 For Service Fees calculated on a time basis and Service Expenses calculated on an actual-cost basis, the Consultant shall keep complete records that clearly identify the relevant service time and Service Expenses, and shall provide such records upon the Client's reasonable request.
