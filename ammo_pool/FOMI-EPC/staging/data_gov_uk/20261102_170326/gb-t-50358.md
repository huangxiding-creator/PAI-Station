## Survey : AMAR 3/94 (part of Solent Bass Pre-recruit Survey (FSS: SOLENT))

*Marine Environmental Data & Information Network*

原文: https://data.gov.uk/dataset/survey-amar-3-94-part-of-solent-bass-pre-recruit-survey-fss-solent1

**摘要/描述摘录:**
> This survey was undertaken by Cefas as part of the Solent Bass Pre-recruit 
> Survey (FSS: SOLENT);

> There are over 50 surveys in this series which began in 1983. The aim of these 
> are to provide abundance indices of 2-4 year old bass. The catch density of 
> other species taken is also recorded. The survey was undertaken twice yearly 
> during May and September for most years up to 2009. Since then one September 
> survey each year has taken place.

> Survey took place between 19/09/1994 and 23/09/1994 on Angelle Marie

> Equipment used during this survey :


> -   Otter Trawl Solent Bass Trawl 5 fm Headline, 6 & 8 metre bridles, 40mm 
>     Liner

> Survey operations were undertaken on 54 stations

> 46 different species were caught on this survey
