Get started for free
You can pay with credit card and will be charged monthly or annually. Read will email you a receipt after a successful payment to the email address you used to create your account. At this time we do not accept PayPal.
How many users can we have with a Pro/Enterprise plan?
All our licenses are individual. If you want to create a workspace, you will need to buy a license for each person you invite. Free users cannot be part of a Workspace, so you will not be able to manage any users without a paid license from your Workspace Settings.
We do not offer admin-only licenses, if you do not plan on using Read we suggest you assign one of the users to be the workspace owner so they can manage licenses and payments.
Can I buy Enterprise licenses for some users and Pro licenses for other users in my workspace?
No, all licenses in a Workspace have to be on the same plan.
I have a paid plan. Can I just share it with others so their meetings can be covered, too?
Read can discount based on the following annual license counts: 100+ licenses:  10% discount, 500+ licenses:  15% discount, 1,000+ licenses:  20% discount
